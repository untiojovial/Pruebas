# I2 notes: Haer. 2.28-2.35 hard passages

**Searched**
- Harvey 1857 Latin/Greek, archive.org `sanctiirenaeiep01harvgoog` (this scan is vol. 1: Book 1 at p.91, Book 2 from c.p.250). Read via djvu OCR; checked on page images for printed pp.351, 353, 355, 357, 368, 387.
- PDF `dl/harvey1857_sanctiirenaeiep01.pdf` (35.8 MB) has no text layer after p.1, so pdftotext cannot verify pages. PDF page = printed page + 224, confirmed at printed 340, 351, 353, 355, 357, 368, 387.
- Migne PG 7 OCR (archive.org): matches the Greek of 2.28.3.
- ANF vol. 1 (Roberts & Rambaut), New Advent 0103228, 0103230, 0103235: English read verbatim; no printed pagination.
- Harvey's chapter numbers (CAP. XLI-LVIII, Grabe/Stieren) differ from Massuet/ANF; margins give "MASS. II" numbers.

**Negative results**
- The brief's "Word and Spirit, who are always with Him" is not in 2.30.9. The Latin is "per Verbum et per Sapientiam suam"; "semper coexsistens" refers to the Son.
- No surviving Greek for 2.28.4, 2.28.6, 2.30.9, 2.35.3-4. Greek in Harvey's notes (p.353 n.6 "BILL.", p.355 n.3, p.387 n.3 Grabe) is retroversion.
- Only Greek surviving in 2.28: 2.28.3 (quoted by John of Damascus, as printed by Harvey).
- Not checked: Greek NT Mark 13:32; the Damascene text itself; Pfaff fragments (not used); scholarly defence (Bull, Newman, Behr etc.), so no defence claims are made.

**Double-check**
- OCR-only Latin: printed pp.349-350 (items 01-03), p.354 (item 07, first sentence), p.356 (item 12, last words). PDF pages 573, 574, 578, 580 are inferred, not visually read.
- Greek verb in 2.28.3: printed "ἀνακείσεται" (read on scan, PDF 575); Harvey n.2 says the Latin "commendamus" implies ἀναθήσομεν, GR. variant ἀνατιθέμεθα.
- Hebrew names in 2.35.3 (pp.384-386): OCR garbled ("Elos verum" / "Elōeim").
- Harvey's "(secundum Latinitatem)" at p.387 is the translator's insertion (n.1).
- 2.28.8: Harvey n.5 says the Clermont MS. omits "Pater" before "major me est".
- "Father greater" (2.28.8) and "a solo Deo Patre" (2.35.4) are the hardest texts for a Trinitarian reading; neither has been answered against a source.
