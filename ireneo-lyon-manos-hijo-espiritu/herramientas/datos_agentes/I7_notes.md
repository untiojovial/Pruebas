# I7 notes: Adversus haereses Book V and Fragments

**Searched and read**
- ANF vol. 1 Book V, all chapters (newadvent.org/fathers/0103500.htm to 0103536.htm), English read in full for the cited paragraphs.
- ANF Fragments (newadvent.org/fathers/0134.htm), read in full.
- Harvey 1857 vol. II, Latin and Greek fragments (archive.org sanctiirenaeili00irengoog, text-layer PDF saved as dl/irenili00_text.pdf, 582 pp.). Page mapping is by pdftotext on that PDF.

**Corrections to the brief**
- "5.1.1 ... transcendent love" is the Book V preface (praefatio), not 5.1.1. Entry I7-01.
- 5.20.1 is the rule-of-faith summary; the hard line is 5.20.2 "ipse caput spiritus factus est" (I7-10).
- 5.36.2 is correct: "per Spiritum ad Filium, per Filium ad Patrem", attributed to the presbyters.
- The brief's 5.1.1 Greek (Hippolytus/Theodoret) was not found. Theodoret's Greek is for 5.1.2 (PDF 326), not read further.

**Negative results**
- No Greek for 5.1.1, 5.18.1-2, 5.20.1, 5.28.4 "manus", or the 5.36.2 ascent sentence in Harvey.
- Pfaff fragments 52-54 (ANF): no Latin or Greek in Harvey 1857 vol. II (Fragmenta, PDF 505-531). Harvey says the Pfaff Turin copy disappeared (PDF 517).
- Fragment 8 (ark, Word and Spirit): Greek in Harvey, cited by Leontius per Harvey's note; Harvey treats it as probably genuine.
- Not checked: Migne PG 7, Stieren, Robinson, Bull, Newman, scholarly defences.

**Harvey's own notes (not Irenaeus)**
- PDF 384 n.: "The Holy Spirit ... proceedeth from the Father and the Son."
- PDF 345 n.: "clear reference to ... the Ever-Blessed Trinity" (5.6.1).
