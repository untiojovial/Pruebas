# I3 notes: Adv. haer. 3.1-3.15

**Searched.** ANF vol. 1 Book III ch. 1-15 on New Advent (Roberts and Rambaut; all sections read). Latin from Harvey 1857 vol. 2 (archive.org `sanctiirenaeiep00harvgoog`, djvu OCR). Page images and text layer from `sanctiirenaeili00irengoog_text.pdf` (saved in `dl/`). Greek from Harvey's apparatus: Eusebius HE 5.8 (3.1.1) and Anastasius Q.144 (3.11.8). Page images checked for 17 of 21 items; I3-02 partly.

**Pagination.** Printed page = PDF page - 11 for pp. 2-73 (checked on images). OCR misreads headers (e.g. "90" for 30, "78" for 73), so check the image.

**Numbering.** Harvey's chapters 9-11 do not match ANF. Harvey Cap. XI sec. 1 is ANF 3.10.1 (Luke); Harvey Cap. IX sec. 2 is the second half of ANF 3.9.1; Harvey Cap. X is unnumbered. Each item gives both.

**Corrections to the brief.**
- "the Father and the Son are rightly called God" is not verbatim. Latin: "Utrosque enim Dei appellatione signavit Spiritus" (3.6.1). "Merito ... Domini appellatione" calls them Lord.
- "Father of all is called God" is 3.6.5, not 3.8.3.
- 3.11.8 has the four-Gospels argument. Its "spiritus" are four winds; "spiritus vitae" is the Gospel. "Uno spiritu" is ambiguous.
- 3.12-3.15: no doctrine of the Spirit's person. The Spirit appears only as promised gift or agent (3.12.1, 3.12.5, 3.12.14-15, 3.14.2). No Spirit mention in 3.13 or 3.15.
- The 3.11.8 Greek is Anastasius's version, with Irenaeus's name omitted and words changed (Harvey n.4). Not an autograph.

**Negative results.** No Greek of Irenaeus's own wording for Trinitarian terms in these chapters; no "consubstantial" or "eternal generation" formula. Pfaff fragments not used (outside range).
