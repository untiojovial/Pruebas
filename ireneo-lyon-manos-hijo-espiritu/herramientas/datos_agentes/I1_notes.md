**Searched.** Harvey 1857 vol. 1 (archive.org `sanctiirenilibr01irengoog`: djvu OCR, PDF, page_numbers.json). Page checks were made by eye on rendered PDF pages (PDF page = printed leaf + 1 here). ANF Book I and II (newadvent 0103100-0103131, 0103200-0103235) were downloaded. Consulted (relevant paragraphs): I.2, I.8, I.9, I.10, I.22, I.30, II.2, II.13, II.19, II.25, II.26. II.14 and II.17 were only partly read.

**Not done.** The brief asks for every Latin sentence naming Father, Word and Spirit in I and II.1-27. That was not achieved. Not read: I.1, I.3-7, I.11-21, I.23-29; II.1, II.3-12, II.15-18, II.20-24, II.27. No scholarly literature was opened.

**Negative results.**

- The "Word and Spirit" pairing is in 1.22.1 (p. 189).
- The Pfaff fragments were not used.
- Latin for 2.17 was not checked.

**Brief corrections.**

- 2.13: "all mind, all Logos, all light" combines 2.13.3 (totus lumen; Latin "sensus" = Greek νοῦς) and 2.13.8 (totus Nus et totus Logos).
