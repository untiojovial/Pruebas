## Negative results
- The phrase "three articles" does not occur. The text says "three points" (sec. 6).
- Brief item 10 ("the Father, the Son, the Spirit" as "Word and Wisdom") is not the wording of sec. 10. Sec. 10 reads "glorified by His Word ... and by the Holy Spirit"; "Word and Wisdom" are the powers.
- Sec. 43: Robinson says the Jeremiah attributions are composites or not in any known book (intro pp. 10ff., 22).
