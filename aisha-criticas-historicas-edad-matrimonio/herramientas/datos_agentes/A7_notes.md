# A7 notes: Latin sources c. 700-1200 (Aisha, age, wives)

## Searched (OCR of Internet Archive Migne PL volumes, full text)
PL 96 (Mozarabic/Isidore Pacensis chronicle 754; Chronicorum Isidori), 115 (Eulogius, Alvarus notes), 121 (Alvarus, Indiculus luminosus), 129 (Anastasius), 141 (Adémar), 156 (Guibert), 157 (Peter Alfonsi), 160 (Sigebert), 171 (Historia de Mahumete), 189 (Peter the Venerable), 212. Also Bibliander, Machumetis Saracenorum principis (Basel 1543; McGill copy on archive.org), which holds Corpus Toletanum texts and Vives. Spellings: Aisa/Aysa/Aiffa/Ayscha/Haisa/Hayssa/Aixa/Axa/Eysa, Abubacer/Eubokara, Zaynab/Zeinab, Khadija/Cadiga.

## Found
- Chronica mendosa (Corpus Toletanum, c. 1142-43, transl. Hermann of Dalmatia): "ad Ayſca filiam Eubokarae coniugem prophetae" (printed p. 220, verified on page image). Earliest Latin naming of Aisha found. NAME, no age.
- Vives (1543, Bibliander): "Dixit etiam Aiſſa uxor Machumet" (p. 10 of section, verified on page image). NAME.
- Vives sexual passage (excluded): names Aysa in an adultery narrative; "adhuc iuvenculam" appears but is unverified (OCR garbled).
- Eulogius, Peter Alfonsi, Peter the Venerable, Guibert, Alvarus, Hildebert, Chronicon Isidori: Muhammad's wives (Zaynab, Khadija, eighteen wives), no Aisha.

## Not found / negative
- No Latin "AGE" passage before 1543. No Latin child-bride or age statement located.
- Eulogius and Paulus Alvarus: no Aisha; "Mahomad" is the Cordoban emir.
- Anastasius: Chronographia tripartita text not located in PL 129 OCR.
- Mozarabic chronicle 754 (PL 96): Muhammad named, no wives.
- Sigebert: only "secta Mahometh" (902).
- Adémar (PL 141): no Muhammad found.
- Neckam: not found in PL 212 OCR.
- Liber generationis Mahumet and Hermann's Doctrina: no wives, no Aisha.
- Robert of Ketton's Qur'an (Bibliander): no Aisha. Epistola Saraceni and Fabulae Saracenorum: not in this volume.
- Embrico's Vita Mahumeti: no public-domain text reached.
- Walter of Compiègne, Otia: only Lepage 1977 and CSIC 2015 editions (copyright). Not quoted.
- Documenta Catholica Omnia: TLS failure. Gallica: 403 (policy). Not routed around.
