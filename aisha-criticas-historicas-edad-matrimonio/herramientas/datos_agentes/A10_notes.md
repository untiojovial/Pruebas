## Spellings searched

## Found
- Earliest explicit age in this set: Prideaux 1698, p. 54: "but six Years old... full eight Years old" (betrothal, consummation), explained as custom of hot countries (not criticised). p. 158: "not then full twenty Years old", then "how much he made his Imposture serve his Lust".
- Reland 1705 p. 181: Aisha ("Aifcha") the only unmarried wife; no age.
- Gagnier 1732, neutral: seven at marriage, nine at consummation.
- Boulainvilliers 1730 (OCR): "fort jeune", no number.
- Marracci 1698: "Aifa" named only in the war narrative; no age.

## Not found (negative)
- No age for Aisha in Sale, Bayle (wives Chadiga and Aafa; the "nubile" remark concerns Mary the Copt), Pococke (wives "17, some say"), Hottinger, du Ryer, Ross, Postel, Bibliander, Luther (no Aisha name in OCR; polygamy and lust polemic only, pp. 130, 169).
