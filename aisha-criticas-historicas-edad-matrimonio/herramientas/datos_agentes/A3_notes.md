# A3 notes: Muslim polemical and theological texts (to AD 1500) on Muhammad's wives and Aisha's age

## Searched
- OpenITI via oilib, about 45 texts read or scanned: Ibn Qutayba (Taʾwil mukhtalif al-hadith; Mushkil al-Quran), Jahiz (Hujaj), Ibn Hazm (Fisal; Muhalla), Ibn Taymiyya (Jawab al-sahih), Ibn Qayyim (Zad; Hidayat al-hayara), Ibn Kathir (Bidaya; Tafsir), Ibn Hajar (Fath), Nawawi (Minhaj), Qadi Iyad (Ikmal), Qurtubi (Mufhim), Razi, Tabari, Mawardi, Abd al-Jabbar (Tathbit), Baqillani, Suyuti (Khasaʾis), Ibn al-Jawzi (Talbis), Muʾayyad, Sijistani, Faryabi, Qawwam al-Sunna, Abu Nuʿaym.
- Spellings: normalized عائشة/عايشة; age words (ست/تسع سنين, بنت تسع); accusers (طعن، اعترض، شبهة، الملحدين، النصارى، اليهود، الزنادقة); marriage and lust words (نكاح، تزوج، ازواج، شهوة).
- Greek, Latin, Syriac spellings: not searched (Muslim scope).

## Not found or not usable
- No Arabic Muslim text read records an explicit age-based objection. Age statements found are neutral or legal.
- Ibn Taymiyya: no wives or age discussion; the "Paul" passages are Christian history.
- Ibn Qayyim, Hidayat: Aisha only as a transmitter of knowledge.
- Qarafi, al-Ajwiba al-fakhira: not in the corpus index.
- Abu Hatim al-Razi, Aʿlam al-nubuwwa (0322AbuHatimRazi.AclamNubuwwa.Noorlib): corpus URL returns 404; not read.
- Qurtubi, al-Jamiʿ li-ahkam al-Quran: not found under title search.
- Ibn al-Rawandi and Abu Bakr al-Razi: no objection texts with wives in the corpus texts scanned.

## Earliest finds
- Marriage slander, not age: Ibn Hazm, Fisal 1:69, reports that Ahmad ibn Habit's followers charged the Prophet with slander "by marriage" (P03). Ahmad's date unverified.
- Age, Muslim: Ibn Hazm, Muhalla 9:40, gives six at marriage (P05). Fisal 4:112 gives eight at the Hijra (P04). Both neutral or legal.
- Christian, outside brief: Kindi reply (9th c.; header claims 861 CE) calls Aisha the only virgin and youngest wife (P01).
