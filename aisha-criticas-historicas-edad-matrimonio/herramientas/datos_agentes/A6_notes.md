# A6 notes: Greek Byzantine sources on Muhammad's wives, Aisha and age

**Read (OCR of archive.org scans; page images not checked):**
- Theophanes, Chronographia, de Boor 1883, pp. 333-334 (AM 6122), with Classen 1839 Bonn (Greek and Latin). Khadija marriage; Abu Bakr as successor. No Aisha.
- PG 104, Bartholomaeus Edessenus, Elenchus and Contra Muhammed (Greek and Latin). Wives list naming "Aise" (Greek OCR "ΑϊσΕ"); Zayd episode; four wives and concubines.
- PG 105, Nicetas of Byzantium, Refutatio. Greek OCR unusable; Latin only. No Aisha, no wife named, no age.
- PG 130, Euthymios Zigabenos, Panoplia XXVIII. Latin read; Greek partly. Four wives and concubines; no Aisha.
- PG 140, Nicetas Choniates, Thesaurus XX. Greek and Latin. Four wives, concubines, Zayd episode; no Aisha.
- PG 154, John VI Kantakouzenos, Contra Mahometem. Latin only. No wife named, no age.
- Bonn Glykas (1836, Greek grep): no Muhammad named. Bonn Chalkokondyles (1843, Latin grep): no Muhammad named.
