# A5 notes: Syriac, Arabic-Christian, Coptic, Armenian, Jewish sources c. 800-1300

## Searched and read
- **Apology of al-Kindi**: OpenITI Kalema transcription (quotes checked with oilib.verify); OpenITI SyriacStudies variant; archive.org AlKindi_201306 (1885 printing, OCR). English: Muir 1882 (archive.org apologyofalkindy00abdarich); book pp. 9-10 checked as image.
- **Timothy I** (Mingana 1928, tertullian.org): full text searched for wife/wives/Aisha/virgin/lust. Nothing on Muhammad's wives.
- **Michael the Syrian**: Chabot French vols 2 (Book XI ch. 2, pp. 405-407) and 3; Langlois 1868 (Armenian version). No Aisha, no age.
- **Bar Hebraeus**: Latin Chronicon ecclesiasticum vols 1-3 searched, nothing on Muhammad's marriages. Latin Chronicon Syriacum (Bruns-Kirsch 1789, OCR): Muhammad narrative read; Khadija named, no Aisha; Abu Bakr only as "father-in-law".
- **Ibn Taymiyya, Jawab al-sahih** (OpenITI): quotes Paul of Antioch's letter; its six chapter headings read, none on wives; section searched for wife terms, none. Aisha hits are hadith only.
- **Ibn Hazm, al-Risala al-Bahira**: about taqlid, not the Bahira legend.
- **Kirakos** (Brosset 1870 French): no Aisha or wives. Armenian 1865 OCR: no Mahmet/Aisha hits (coverage unclear).
- **Maimonides**: partial Wikisource page only (no wives).
