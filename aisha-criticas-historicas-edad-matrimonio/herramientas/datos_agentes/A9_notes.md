# A9 notes (Juan Andrés, Iberian tradition)

## Read at page level
- 1515 Valencia Confusión (Spanish): LOC Rosenwald copy, LCCN 47043529. Read from the scan images (LOC IIIF index 182 and 183 of 189). The LOC OCR was used only to locate the passage; it is unreliable (f for long s).
  - "siendo ella de seys años" (betrothal at six); "siendo ella de ocho años" (consummation at eight); "libro de acear" (the source, = Azar); "siete mugeres juntamente"; "vna mochacha de ocho años".
- 1652 English (EEBO-TCP A74637, CC0), read from the GitHub XML mirror (quod.lib.umich.edu returns 403): pp. 229-230 by TCP page breaks say six and eight, "Axa ... Ubequar's Daughter", "book Azar". p. 117: fifteen wives married, eleven together, two virgins (English only).
- Szpiech, La Corónica 2012 (PDF): about Paul, no Aisha passage. Gives edition history (four 16th-c. Castilian editions; at least 15 later editions in six languages).
- Espina, Fortalitium fidei (Internet Archive 1487 OCR): no hits for Aisha spellings or "uxor" in a Muhammad context. OCR very poor; generic "libidinosa" phrases are garbled. Needs image check.
