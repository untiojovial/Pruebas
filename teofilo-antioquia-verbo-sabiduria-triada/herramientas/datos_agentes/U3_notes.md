# U3 notes: Ad Autolycum II.10-12 (plus II.22)

## Searched
- English: Dods, ANF vol. 2, Book II, https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/02042.htm. Every English quote in passages.json was checked as an exact substring of that page.
- Greek: Migne PG 6 (1857), archive.org item patrologiae_cursus_completus_gr_vol_006_justin_et_al_version1, djvu.txt OCR (dl/pg6_v1_djvu.txt). Read 2.10-12, 2.22, and the 2.15 Triad line.
- Otto 1861 (Corpus apologetarum vol. 8): not found on archive.org by title or identifier search. Sandig 1969 reprints (corpusapologeta00-03): 00 and 01 are Latin only; 02/03 have Greek but not Theophilus. Wikisource (en) returned 404.
- PG 6 PDF is 83.7 MB, over the 60 MB limit, so not downloaded. page_file is null.

## Negative results
- hegemon (ἡγεμ-): absent from Book II Greek.
- Prov 8:22 (ἔκτισ-): absent from Book II. Prov 8:27 and 8:30 are quoted in 2.10 instead.
- Κύριος: absent from 2.12. It first appears in ch. 22.
- "Ever innate in the heart of God" is in 2.22, not 2.10 as the brief says.
- In 2.10 the Word is called ἀρχή (governing principle). The "beginning" of Gen 1:1 is a noun for creation, not the Word.
