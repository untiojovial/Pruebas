# U10 notes: Apologist parallels

**Searched.** New Advent ANF pages for Justin (1 Apol., 2 Apol., Dial. 61-62, 128), Tatian, Athenagoras, Irenaeus 4.20, Hippolytus Contra Noetum, Tertullian Adv. Prax., Origen De princ. 1.1-1.2. Greek from archive.org scans: Otto's Justin (1842; 1843 OCR only), Otto's Athenagoras (1857), Otto's Tatian (1969 reprint, OCR only), Hippolytus Werke vol. 3. Latin: Kroymann CSEL 47 (Tertullian), Harvey 1857 (Irenaeus, OCR only).

**Negative results.**
- Justin's text as read does not use endiathetos or prophorikos. Those words appear only in Otto's Latin notes.
- Tatian Oratio 5 and Athenagoras Legatio 10 do not use them (zero OCR hits).
- Contra Noetum 10: Greek not located; the English uses no Greek term. The Greek endiathetos passage is Elenchos X.33 (U10-15), a rival teaching Hippolytus refutes.
- Irenaeus Book IV survives only in Latin. 2 Apol. 6 Greek not located.
- Origen: only De princ. 1.1-1.2 read.

**Corrections to the brief.**
- The "unity in power, distinction in order" sentence is Athenagoras Legatio 10, not 12. Chapter 12 concerns two kinds of spirits.
- "Father, Son and Holy Ghost" in Athenagoras 10 is the ANF heading, not Athenagoras's text.
- The real Spirit/Wisdom parallels are Justin 1 Apol. 13 and Irenaeus 4.20.3.
