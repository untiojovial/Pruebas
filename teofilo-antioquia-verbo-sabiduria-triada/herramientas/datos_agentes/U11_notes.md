# U11 notes: Theophilus in the tradition

## Searched and found
- Jerome, De viris 25 (newadvent 2708, Richardson): three books To Autolycus; doubts the commentaries by style. Latin not read.
- Eusebius HE 4.20.1, 4.24.1, 4.24.3 (newadvent 250104, McGiffert). Greek not read.
- Lactantius, Div. Inst. 1.23 (ANF 7): cites Theophilus on chronology; editor's note says no extant work bears that title.
- Tertullian, Adv. Prax. 6-7 (ANF 3; Latin via earlychurchtexts/Migne PL 2): Prov 8 as second Person. Does not name Theophilus.
- Newman note p. 214 (newmanreader.org): transliterated Ad Autol. 2.10-22; Bull's answer (Defensio III.5-9) as quoted.
- NPNF2 vol. 4 (ccel): editors' notes on "trias" (2.15) and endiathetos/prophorikos (2.10, 2.22). Secondary.
- Ad Autolycum 2.10, 2.15, 2.22 (Dods, ANF 2).

## Negative results
- No Arian text in Athanasius cites Theophilus: Thalia, Arius's letter, Asterius, Arian creeds, Sirmium 351 creed (De Synodis 8), Contra Arianos. The only "Theophil" hit in De Synodis is Theophronius of Tyana.
- Jerome, Ep. 121 on newadvent holds only the eleven questions. The Theophilus Diatessaron remark is only in the CE article.
- Photius (codex 182), Gennadius (DVI on Theophilus), Gelasian Decree: searched; no text found.
- Marcellus of Ancyra: fourthcentury.com pages returned empty. No source found for a Theophilus citation of "prophorikos". NPNF2 vol. 4 has no Marcellus fragments.
