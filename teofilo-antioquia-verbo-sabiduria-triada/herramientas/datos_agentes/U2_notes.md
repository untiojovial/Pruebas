## Negative results
- Book II ch. 1-9: no "Word", "Logos", "Triad", "Trinity", "homoousios" in English or Greek. No "Hermogenes" in Book II at all.
- Ch. 4 and ch. 7 do not make the Logos or Wisdom God's instrument.
- Brief correction: "by His own Word" is not in 2.4. The "out of nothing" statement is in 2.10 (Greek "ἐξ οὐκ ὄντων τὰ πάντα ἐποίησεν"), and "by Him He made all things" (δι' αὐτοῦ) is also at 2.10. 2.4 says only "out of things that are not".
- The only instrument passage in 1-9 is 2.9 (U2-09, U2-10): prophets as "ὄργανα Θεοῦ", receiving wisdom "through which" they spoke.
