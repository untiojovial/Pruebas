# U5 notes: Ad Autolycum 2.19-27 (plus 2.9, 2.18, 2.33)

## Searched and used
- English: ANF vol. 2, Dods, New Advent Book II (https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/02042.htm). All English strings in passages.json match this text.
- Greek (primary): Otto, Corpus apologetarum vol. VIII, Sändig 1969 facsimile, Internet Archive item corpusapologeta01moragoog (PDF in dl/, 441 pages). Printed page 118 = PDF 184. Greek and the Latin notes were read from 300-dpi page images. The embedded OCR of the Greek is unusable.
- Greek (cross-check): BKV (bkv.unifr.ch) prints Grant 1970 with angle-bracket supplements. Grant brackets δύναμις, καὶ σοφία, and πρωτότοκον πάσης κτίσεως, which Otto prints without brackets.

## Negative or failed routes

- bub_gb_JCcjjjP6NP8C (Fell 1684): Latin only, no Greek in OCR.
- Migne PG 6: the Harvard "patrologiaecurs" items are whole-series scans; not searched page by page. Not verified.
- Perseus/Scaife CTS API: TLS error; Perseus GitHub XML: 404.
- Grant 1970 itself: not openly accessible. BKV used only as a transcription check.

## English-text searches (ANF Book II)
- Capitalised "Son": once in Book II, in 2.22. Lowercase "sons" occurs elsewhere (sons of gods, Cain's line).
- "Holy Spirit/Ghost": 2.9 (heading and text), 2.30, 2.33. None in 2.19-27.
- "Triad": 0 hits. ANF renders trias as "Trinity" in 2.15 ("types of the Trinity"), so the English word "Triad" is a translator's choice.
- "homoousios": 0 hits in English (Greek not searched for it).
