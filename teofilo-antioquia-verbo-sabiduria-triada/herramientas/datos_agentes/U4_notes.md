## Negative results

- 2.13: Spirit is "that which God gave" to animate creation; Wisdom is not named there.
- 2.16: the BKV Greek has a blank where ANF reads "the manifold wisdom of God". Wisdom in 2.16 is unverified in Greek.
- 2.17: no Word, Wisdom, Spirit or Trinity term.
- 2.14: church imagery only ("holy churches").
- Pages: no printed page reached; all items say "page unknown". No PDF page verified.
