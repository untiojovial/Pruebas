# S1 notes: primary sources on the 1299 marriage of Simonis and Milutin

## Searched and read
- archive.org Bonn volumes (OCR text, then page images rendered from the Google PDFs, which are JBIG2 scans with no text layer):
  - Gregoras, Bonn vol. 1 (nicephorigregor00bekkgoog): pp. 203, 204, 243 read as images; pp. 467-468 read as OCR only.
  - Gregoras vol. 2 (nicephorigregor01bekkgoog): editorial note by Boivin/Possinus on p. 204 (Milutin 'quadragenarius', Possinus argues ~45).
  - Pachymeres, Bonn vol. 2 (georgiipachymer00pousgoog): pp. 273, 275, 279, 280, 285 read as images; Possinus chronological table p. 849 (1299) and p. 850 (1300) read as image.
- Did NOT find or read: Metochites on this embassy (none in Gregoras or Pachymeres as read); Danilo II's Life of Milutin (not accessed); Greek of Pachymeres p. 276 'nubilem aetatem' (Latin only).

## Negative results
- No 'wait until twelve' condition in Gregoras VI.9 or Pachymeres (both say 'customary/marriageable time'). The 'twelve' in Gregoras is a different bride (IX.13).
- Gregoras does not say consummation was 'immediate'; he gives no date for it.
- Gregoras gives Milutin's age as 'forty' (quadragenarius), not 'fifty'.
- No Metochites embassy evidence in these two chronicles.

## For the orchestrator to double-check
1. Draft's 'Gregoras says EIGHT': confirmed (VII.5, p. 243), but the sentence is Gregoras's later account of the intercourse and sterility, not a statement about the 1299 wedding date.
2. 'Milutin about fifty': Gregoras says 40; the 'nearly fifty' is Possinus's editorial summary (Bonn p. 849), not Gregoras or Pachymeres.
3. Pachymeres' book numbering: printed running head reads 'L. III' on p. 275, but Possinus's note cites 'lib. II cap. XXXI'. Check before citing a book number.
4. Possinus's 1300 summary (p. 850) says the patriarch lived privately at Pammakaristos after the marriage; I did not read the Greek of that chapter.
5. The Achrida prelate's name (Παχαρίου or Μαχαρίου) is not clear on the scan; check the page image before naming him.
6. Greek of Gregoras IX.13 (12-year-old bride) is OCR only.
