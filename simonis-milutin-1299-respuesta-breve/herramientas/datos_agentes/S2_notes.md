## Double-check
1. "Eight" is in Gregoras' own Greek (P3), not only in Georgievska-Shine. The "before legal age" gloss is not in Gregoras.
2. "Wait until twelve" is not in Gregoras: he says "until the customary age of marriage" (P4).
3. Loophole: Pachymeres gives the mechanism (lawful wife's death, P8). Gregoras says the first wife was sent home alive (P8, P9). Fine's wording unverified.
4. Kosmas: Pachymeres shows an unnamed patriarch opposing (P7). Naming him needs Failler.
5. The Lascaratos abstract does not name Simonis (P1).
6. Greek quotes are OCR-normalised; re-check before print.
7. Milutin's age: Gregoras says about forty and about five years older than the emperor; Gregorovius and Pachymeres' editors say 45; the draft says almost fifty.
