# A5 notes: Legatio 10, 24-27

## Searched
- ANF vol. 2 (Pratten) at newadvent.org/fathers/0205.htm: chapters 10, 24-27 read in full. English quotes are pulled by script from that text.
- Otto, CAC vol. VII (Jena 1857; Sandig 1969 reprint), archive.org corpusapologeta02unkngoog. Used the text-layer PDF (557 pp.) and the djvu OCR. Greek of Legatio pp. 46-50 and 120-144 located. Image checked for pp. 46, 48, 50, 122, 124, 126, 128, 130, 132, 138, 142 and Latin p. 125.
- Whole Otto text searched for Ἰησ-, Χριστ-, ὁμοούσ-, τριάδ/τριάς, τρία.

## Negative results
- Ἰησοῦς: not in the Greek text. Only editors' names in the Latin prolegomena.
- Χριστός: only in titles (Χριστιανοί) and in Otto's notes quoting Eusebius. Not in Athenagoras's body text.
- ὁμοούσιος: absent.
- τριάς / Trinitas: absent from the Greek. Latin commentary only.
- τρία: only in Gesner's rejected conjecture (p. 125, note 6) and in a Plato quotation in a note.
- De resurrectione: not checked in this assignment.
