# A6 notes: Legatio 28-37

**Searched**
- Greek: Otto, Corpus Apologetarum VII (Jena 1857), archive.org `athenagoraephilo00athe`. Full PDF text layer (418 pp.), with page images checked at pdf 242, 244, 248, 250, 252, 258, 268. Printed Greek page = pdf page minus 84.
- English: ANF vol. 2 (Pratten), newadvent.org/fathers/0205.htm. Chapter numbers in Otto and ANF agree (28 = Herodotus, 30 = Callimachus, 37 = prayer).
- Not checked: Migne PG 6, Dods, Schoedel, Marcovich. No second Greek witness.

**Negative results**
- Jesus (`Ἰησ-`): 0 hits in the whole Otto volume text layer.
- Christ (`Χριστ-`, excluding Χριστιαν-): hits only at pdf 17 (praefatio), 72-73 (prolegomena), 133 (apparatus). None in the Legatio's main text, including 28-37.
- English ANF, Legatio: "Jesus" 0; "Christ" as a whole word 0; "Trinity"/"Triad" 0. "Holy Ghost" appears only in the ch. 10 heading.
- `τριάς`/`τριάδ-`: 0. `ὁμοούσιος`: 0.
- "Son" after ch. 12, ch. 28-37: no "Son of God" (υἱὸς τοῦ θεοῦ). Occurrences are pagan (ch. 28-30, Zeus's children in 32), human kin (ch. 32, υἱοὺς καὶ θυγατέρας), and imperial παῖς (ch. 37).
- Holy Spirit (ἅγιον πνεῦμα): none in 28-37. πνεῦμα appears only as the risen "heavenly spirit" (ch. 31).
- "ὁ ἅγιος λόγος" (ch. 32) is Otto's apparatus parallel from Theophilus, not Athenagoras's text. Not used.
- Matthew 5:28 and 19:9 references are added by ANF, not in Athenagoras's Greek. Speaker is "φησί" (unnamed).

**Outside assignment (flag)**
- Leg. 18 (pdf 166-168, printed 82-84): "ἑνὶ τῷ θεῷ καὶ τῷ παρ᾽ αὐτοῦ λόγῳ υἱῷ νοουμένῳ ἀμερίστῳ πάντα ὑποτέτακται". This is the clearest "Son" sentence in this range. Whoever covers ch. 18 should verify it.
