# A4 notes: Legatio 13-26 (Logos, Son, Spirit, angels, creatures)

## Searched
- Otto 1857 (archive.org athenagoraephilo00athe; PDF 418 pp., text layer). Printed page = PDF page - 84 for the Supplicatio. Greek pages are even printed pages; Latin is on the odd pages (facing).
- Greek pages read as images at 160 dpi; key lines re-read at 300-600 dpi crops (Leg. 15, 23, 24 Trinity and angel sentences, giants).
- Migne PG 6 (archive.org, OCR text) confirms the Leg. 24 Trinity sentence; column not legible.
- English: ANF vol. 2 (newadvent 0205.htm), Pratten, read in full for ch. 13-26.

## Negative results
- Greek OCR, PDF pp. 142-232 (ch. 13-27): no Χριστ-, Ἰησ-, Ἰουδ-, τριάδ-, τριάς, ὁμοούσ-.
- English ch. 13-26: "Christ" (whole word) 0, "Jesus" 0, "Trinity"/"Triad" 0.
- "Logos" in English: 2 (ch. 18, 24). Greek υἱός: ch. 18 and 24 only.
- Angels (ἄγγελοι) occur only in ch. 24-25 in this range. Demons: ch. 23, 25, 26.

## Key findings for the deck
- ANF ch. 24 says the Son and Spirit are "united in essence". The Greek is κατὰ δύναμιν ("in power"). Otto's Latin p. 125 says "secundum potentiam", and Migne agrees. Do not quote the ANF phrase as support for homoousios.
- ANF ch. 18 "proceeding" is not in the Greek (παρ' αὐτοῦ).
- Leg. 24 ἀρχούσας is Otto's conjecture (Maranus); the codices read ἔχουσας (Otto's note 9 on p. 124).
- Leg. 23 Plato "two and three" passage: ANF's "moving" is not in the Greek.
