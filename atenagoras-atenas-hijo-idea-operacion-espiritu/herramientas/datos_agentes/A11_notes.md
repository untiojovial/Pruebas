# A11 notes: reception and parallels (Athenagoras)

## Searched and found
- Otto, Corpus Apologetarum vol. VII prolegomena (archive.org corpusapologeta03moragoog, OCR text-layer PDF p. 266 = printed p. xiii): Paris gr. 451 (914, written by Baanes, notary of Arethas of Caesarea) contains Embassy and De resurrectione. VERIFIED (Latin, OCR).
- Eusebius HE books 1-9 (newadvent 250101-250109): no "Athenagoras". Book 4 names Quadratus and Aristides only. Book 10 page did not load.
- Jerome De vir. ill. (NPNF2 full list, 135 chapters): no Athenagoras chapter. Ch. 23 (Justin) says Justin addressed a work to Marcus Aurelius and Commodus, which is the Embassy's addressees (ambiguous, as the DCB notes).
- Philip of Side fragment 2 (tertullian.org, English): Athenagoras head of Alexandrian school before Pantaenus; Eusebius contradicts (secondary).
- ANL introduction: only two early references, Methodius (via Epiphanius Haer. 64 and Photius Bibl. 234) and Philip.
- Parallels read in English (ANF): Justin Dial. 61, 128; 1 Apol. 6, 13; Tatian Or. 5; Theophilus 2.10, 2.15, 2.22; Irenaeus 4.20.1/3; Tertullian Adv. Prax. 2, 8.
