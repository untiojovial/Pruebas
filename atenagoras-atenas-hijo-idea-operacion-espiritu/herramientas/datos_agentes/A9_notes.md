# A9 notes: scholarship on Athenagoras's Trinity and Logos

## Searched and read (text layers from archive.org scans; PDFs in aq/A9/dl/)
- Newman, Arians of the Fourth Century, 7th ed. 1890 (pp. 170-172, 198).
- Bull, Defensio, Parker 1851 English (pp. 53, 152-154); Greek footnotes read from OCR.
- Bethune-Baker, 1903 (pp. 46, 128-129, 200); index entry p.128.
- Dorner, Div. I vol. I, 1861 (pp. 283-284; Notes p.393).
- Harnack, History of Dogma vol. I, Buchanan (p.346).
- Loofs, Leitfaden, 4th ed. 1906 (German: pp. 121, 122, 126). Only the djvu text was used: the PDF is 90 MB, over the 60 MB limit.
- ANF/Pratten English for Plea and De resurrectione (newadvent 0205, 0206).

## Negative results
- No "Jesus" and no whole-word "Christ" in the Pratten English of the Plea or De resurrectione; "Christians" is frequent. Dorner (p.283) makes the same claim. Greek not checked.
- No "Trinity" or "Triad" in the English of either work. Greek "τριάς" not checked.
- No "homoousios" or "consubstantial" in the English of either work.
- Bull's "consubstantial" is his own gloss, not an Athenagoras word.

## Not accessible (archive.org 401, lending copies)

## Must double-check
1. Greek: Bull's Greek (p.152 n.c, p.153 n.i) and Dorner's (p.284 n.1) were read from OCR; Greek of Leg. 10 must be compared with Otto/Schwartz (BB names Schwartz 1891).
2. Leg. 10 vs. Leg. 24: in ANF the "angels and ministers" sentence sits in the same passage as the effluence, so it is Leg. 10 and not Leg. 24 as the brief supposes. Check in Otto.
3. Bull's Book/chapter numbering (II iv sec. 9) and Loofs's German OCR ('Kausiertsein' unclear).
4. Pratten renders πρὸς αὐτοῦ as "after the pattern of Him and by Him"; Bull and a Benedictine editor disagree.
5. Dorner's "c. 22" note on the Spirit does not match Leg. 10; not resolved.
