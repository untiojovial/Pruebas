## Key findings
- CA I.20: the Son is "ἀπαύγασμα" (radiance). CA I.21-22 reject "ἀπορροία" (effluence) for the Son; Leg. 10 Spirit-effluence is not addressed.
- Or. 31.6: if the Spirit is an accident, He is an activity (energeia) of God. Or. 31.32 rejects sun-and-ray.
- Adv. Prax. 8: ray and fountain analogies, Spirit third from God and Son.
