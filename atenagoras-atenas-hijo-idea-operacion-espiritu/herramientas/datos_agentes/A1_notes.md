# A1 notes: Legatio 1-9 (+ ch.10 opening, the "one God" formula)

## Searched
- Otto 1857, Corpus Apologetarum vol. 7 (archive.org athenagoraephilo00athe; local PDF dl/otto1857.pdf). Greek printed page = PDF page - 84. Greek read from page images (200-600 dpi) and checked against the PDF text layer.
- ANF vol. 2, Pratten translation: newadvent.org/fathers/0205.htm, ch.1-10 read in full. ANF page numbers come from OCR of the 1926 archive.org reprint (antenicenefather0002unse_s3t5), not checked on a scan.
- Migne PG 6 (archive.org): OCR too poor, mixed with Latin; not used for quotes.
- Otto whole-text OCR searches: "Ἰησοῦ"/"Ἰησ": 0 hits. "Χριστός" in Athenagoras's own text: none (one German note only). "τριάς/τριάδ", "ὁμοούσ", "Trinit": 0 hits. "Logos"/"Spirit"/"Christus" occur only in Otto's notes and index.

## Negative results (Legatio 1-9)
- No "Jesus" and no "Christ"; only Χριστιανοί/Christianus.
- No "Son" (υἱός) in ch.1-9; the Son first appears in ch.10 (A1-13).
- The Spirit is πνεῦμα or πνοή (ch.6-9): God's Spirit holding the cosmos (ch.6), a breath from God (ch.7), the prophetic Spirit as a flute-player (ch.9). Never "Holy Spirit", no person language.
- Logos in ch.4 and 6 is a dative of agency or "the Logos from Him", never a named person.
- ANF's "musical instruments" (ch.7): the Greek has only ὄργανα.
