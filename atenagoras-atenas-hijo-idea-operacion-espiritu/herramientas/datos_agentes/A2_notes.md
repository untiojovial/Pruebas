# A2 notes: Legatio 10 (Athenagoras)

## Searched
- Greek: Otto, Corpus Apologetarum vol. 7 (archive.org corpusapologeta02unkngoog; Sandig reprint 1969 of the 1857 Jena edition). Read the djvu text, the Google text-layer PDF (otto7_text.pdf, 36.6 MB, local dl/), and rendered page images at 110-600 dpi. Ch. 10 = printed pp. 44-50 (Greek on 44, 46, 48, 50; Latin facing on 45, 47, 49). PDF page = printed page + 85.
- Second Greek: Migne PG 6 (archive.org patrologiae_cursus_completus_gr_vol_006), djvu text only; PDF too large (83 MB).
- English: ANF vol. 2, B. P. Pratten, newadvent.org/fathers/0205.htm (chapter 10 read in full). CCEL page-level route not resolved; ANF printed page is therefore "page unknown".

## Negative results (ch. 10 only)
- No Ἰησοῦς, no Χριστός in the Greek of ch. 10. No τριάς/Τριάς, no ὁμοούσιος, no ὑπηρέτης, no ἀπόρροια beyond the one Spirit clause.
- The English of ch. 10 has no "Jesus", "Trinity", "Triad" or "person".
- Whole-work searches for "Jesus"/"Christ" (beyond ch. 10) and for the flute passage (Leg. 9) were not done here.

## Conflicts and OCR doubts (check before slides)
1. ἐν ἰδέα (Otto main text, scan) vs ἐν ἰδέᾳ (Otto note 19 and Migne). Brief says ἰδέᾳ. Look at the scan of printed p. 46.
2. Otto text layer 'εἷς' vs Migne 'ὡς' (Οὐ γὰρ ... ποιηταὶ, A2-02).
3. κόσμου (Migne, genitive) vs scan glyph κόσμον (A2-10).
4. ἀνεκδιηγήτω (Otto OCR) vs ἀνεκδιηγήτῳ (Migne).
5. Otto's ἔστιν ὃ υἱὸς (p. 44 end) is from the text layer only.
6. The ANF bracketed gloss "[I say His Logos]" has no separate Greek clause.
