# A7 notes: De resurrectione (Athenagoras)

**Searched**
- English: ANF vol. 2 (B. P. Pratten), newadvent.org/fathers/0206.htm, all 25 chapters; grep for Christ, Jesus, Son, Spirit, Word, Logos, Trinity, Triad, Holy.
- Greek: Otto, Corpus Apologetarum vol. VII (Jena 1857; Sändig reissue 1969), archive.org corpusapologeta02unkngoog, text layer, PDF 381-518 (printed pp. 186-289). Chapter numbers agree with ANF.
- Migne PG 6: no archive.org identifier found. Wikisource Greek: nothing found.
- Paris gr. 451 (archive.org AthenagorasDeResPlut/ED): image scans, no text layer; not quoted.

**Negative results**
- Greek body: no Χριστ-, Ἰησ-, υἱ-, πνεῦμα, Τριάς, ὁμοούσ-, or capital Λόγος. Θεός appears only as Maker (ποιητής, δημιουργός, πλάσας).
- English: no "Christ", "Jesus", "Son", "Logos", "Trinity", "Holy". "Spirit" only in "Spiritual natures (νοηταὶ φύσεις)".
- No Greek line contains both ποιητ- and λόγ-, so "God the Maker and the Logos" (brief, ch. 13?) is absent. Ch. 13 concerns hope of immortality.
- λόγος means argument or reason; ch. 15 gives νοῦς καὶ λόγος to men as faculties.
- The one "ἅγιον πνεῦμα" in the volume is in Otto's index, pointing to the Legatio.

**Authenticity**
- Ascription: manuscripts, including Paris gr. 451 (914, scribe Baanes for Arethas), per Otto's prolegomena (A7-12).
- Sceptic: Grant 1954 (HTR 47, pp. 121-129, DOI 10.1017/S0017816000027528), who cites Lucks 1936 and Keseling 1950. Read only as a WebFetch summary; PARTLY.
- Not checked: Schoedel, Pouderon, Barnard, Harnack, Loofs, Bull, Newman, Kelly. No memory-only entries recorded.
