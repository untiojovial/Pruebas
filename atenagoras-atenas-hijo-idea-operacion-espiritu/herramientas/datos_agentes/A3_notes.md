# A3 notes: Legatio 10-12

**Searched and read**
- Greek: Otto, Corpus Apologetarum vol. VII (Jena: Mauke, 1857; Sändig reprint 1969), archive.org `corpusapologeta02unkngoog`. Read from page images (PDF pp. 131, 133, 135, 149, 157, 159) at 150-300 dpi, not only OCR. Greek pages are even (46, 48, 50, 52, 54, 56); odd pages are facing Latin. The scan repeats some pages.
- English: ANF vol. 2 (Pratten) at newadvent 0205.htm, read in full. No ANF printed page available (ccel and archive.org ANF copies not usable).
- Paged English: Dods, Ante-Nicene Christian Library vol. 2 (Clark 1867), archive.org `AnteNiceneChristianLibraryV02`. Ch.10 is at pp. 385-386. Ch.11-12 pages not checked.
- Negative searches in the Greek and English texts:
  - "Ἰησοῦς" / "Iesu": none in the Legatio text.
  - "Χριστός": only in Otto's prolegomena and notes (lines quoting Euseb., Justin). The Legatio text uses only "Χριστιανοί".
  - "Christ" in the English: none. "Jesus": none.
  - "τριάς" / "Trinitas": none. "ὁμοούσιος": none.
  - "ἕνα θεόν" (one God): ch.4 and ch.7 only, not ch.11-12.

**Key findings**
- Ch.10 has the Father-Son-Spirit triad (`θεὸν πατέρα καὶ υἱὸν θεὸν καὶ πνεῦμα ἅγιον`), with unity as power and distinction as order (A3-06).
- Ch.12 has a question-list of the three (A3-13): `τίς ἡ τῶν τοσούτων ἕνωσις καὶ διαίρεσις`, with the Spirit listed among them.
- Ch.12 has no "we believe in the One God" formula, despite the brief.
- Otto's note 9 (p.46) reads `ἑνότητι καὶ δυνάμει πνεύματος` as divine essence, not the Holy Spirit as person.
