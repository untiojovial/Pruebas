# T7 notes: al-Jawab al-Sahih, structure and handling of Paul of Antioch's letter

**Searched (JawabSahih, OpenITI 0000170):** بولس، صيدا، الانطاكي، سبب تأليف الكتاب، مجمل ما جاء في رسالة بولس، نهج المؤلف، الفصل الأول/الثاني/…/السادس، ألفاظهم بأعيانها، قالوا، الاقانيم، صفات جوهرية، اليعقوبية/النسطورية/الملكية، الكاتب على لسان الأسقف. Secondary: Bogataj 2019 (DOI 10.34291/Edinost/74/02/Bogataj), Khoury 1964 record, web snippets on Nestorian/Jacobite Christology.

**Found:**
- Occasion: a book from Cyprus (T7-01); the letter is ascribed to Paul, bishop of Sidon of Antioch (T7-02).
- Six sections are listed as a summary (T7-03, T7-04). Answers are under topic headings (vol 1 p.114 onward; vol 1 p.129 '[فصل: توضيح الدعوى...]' for section 1; vol 2 pp.29-290 for the rest).
- Literal Christian text is marked only for section 1 (T7-08, T7-09). Sections 2-6 have no marked literal quotation that I found.
- Method: T7-05, T7-06, T7-07.
- Sects: T7-12 (tafsir, vol 2 p.10-11) and T7-13 (vol 3 p.189).

**Not found / limits:**
- Ibn Taymiyya says the friend was "one of his friends"; he does not say Muslim. The "Muslim friend" wording is from modern sources (Bogataj: Risala ila Ahad al-Muslimin).
- The Christian text is not in quotation marks; attribution to Paul depends on his own text.
- Summary uses "al-aqanim" (hypostases). Bogataj says Paul avoids "uqnum" and uses "name" (ism). Check which wording the Arabic text uses. The Cypriot revision (Bogataj) may explain the difference.
- Paul's Arabic (Khoury 1964) not accessed. All christian_check content comes from Bogataj's English translation (Abdelsadek), which I did not verify.
- Nestorian and Jacobite points come from search snippets only (not read in full).
- Edition: "والعقلية،،" (vol 1 p.98) is a typo. "المريوسية" and "الملافطة" are not identified.
- "famous" is set false for all passages; not checked against citation data.
- Verdicts are candidates only.
