## Key finding for the orchestrator
- The phrase القانون الكلي is NOT Ibn Taymiyya's own rule. In DarTacarud vol. 1 p. 3 he uses قانونا كليا for the rule of Razi and his followers (four options, then "reason first"). His own universal claim is "قولا عاما كليا" (vol. 1 p. 154, T3-05), and his core formula is "صريح المعقول لا يناقض صحيح المنقول" (vol. 2 p. 363, T3-06). Brief wording should be adjusted.
