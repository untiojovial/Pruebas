**Agreements.** Abdullah and Zwirahn agree IT's Trinity case rests on scripture plus reason and on taḥrīf. IT's own text (C03, C07) and Roberts (via Zwirahn) agree Christians gloss "hypostasis" variously.

**Disagreements.**
- Accuracy: Abdullah is sympathetic; Zwirahn reports the accusation that IT misrepresents the Trinity (Roberts 1996, unread).
- Fitra: Rayan & Baraijia say IT's view is inconsistent and ends sensory. A search-snippet summary says fitra apprehends likeness and logical axioms (cited by Rayan, sec. 1). Unresolved.
- Threefold tawhid: IT's own text (T01) gives the kalam threefold (essence/attributes/acts), not rububiyya/uluhiyya/asma. Snippet sources say the uluhiyya/rububiyya terms predate IT. "IT invented the threefold" needs qualifying.
