VERDICTS. (a) Creeds, Basil, Gregory Or.31, Augustine, Damascus, Aquinas, Lateran IV: persons are three subsistences/relations, each God, not attributes. None calls a person an attribute. (b) Attributes language appears in: Basil (common perfections as 'attributes'); Augustine (great/good said of the substance); Arabic: Paul's formula (essence/speech/life, as quoted), Yahya's Zayd analogy (as reported). Husseini (pp. 376, 386) says Abu Ra'ita and Ammar do NOT equate hypostases with attributes. (c) Tritheism is condemned: Athanasian 16-20, Gregory Or.31, Augustine V.8, Lateran IV canons 1-2.

(5) Schaff's Athanasian English is the 1548 BCP text with his brackets. (6) Stance: C17 quotes a Christian text; C18 reports an opponent; C19 and C20 are Ibn Taymiyya's own view (honesty rule; spec said 'Christian source').
