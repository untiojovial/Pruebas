**Negative results**
- "trinitas" does not occur in ch. 1. First occurrence: ch. 2, p. 229 ("unitatem in trinitatem disponit"). Also ch. 3 and ch. 4.
- "monarchia" first occurs in ch. 3, p. 230 (not in ch. 2).
- No Latin for ch. 2's "unius substantiae" outside the CSEL 47 scan.

**Double-check**
1. Kroymann apparatus (p. 231, PDF 271): the editor marks "cum alius deus ... finem capitis" (end of ch. 3, "tunc male, cum plures, secundum Valentinos et Prodicos") as an interpolation. Items T1-08 to T1-10 use text before that stretch. Do not cite the end of ch. 3 as Tertullian without this caveat.
2. Ch. 3 (p. 230): the bracketed sentence "[sed monarchiam sonare student... etiam Graeci.]" is secluded by Kroymann; ANF translates it anyway (T1-07).
3. Ch. 3 (p. 231): "<alienorum>" is Gelenius's supplement; the ANF omits that clause (T1-10).
4. Ch. 4 (p. 232): "deputo" is Kroymann's emendation for MSS "puto" (T1-12). "[filium]" is secluded (not used).
5. Ch. 3 (p. 231): "unitis" is Engelbrecht's reading against MSS "unicis" (T1-08).
6. ANF page numbers are unknown; ANF English is cited by chapter only.
7. Latin in T1-02 to T1-13 was read from the scan images, so OCR errors (e.g. "graclu" for "gradu") were corrected by the scan.
8. Chapter divisions: Kroymann's and ANF's chapter numbers agree.
9. I did not verify the modern scholars' readings (Bull, Newman, Evans, Osborn and others).
