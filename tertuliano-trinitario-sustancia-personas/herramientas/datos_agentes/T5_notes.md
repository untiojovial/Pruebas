## Negative results
- "tertius est spiritus a Deo et Filio" is in ch. 8 (Migne 0164A; ANF p. 603), not ch. 25. Ch. 25 has "tres efficit cohaerentes ... tres unum sint, non unus".
- "Pater passus" is denied in ch. 29. "Pater natus, et Pater passus" is the Praxean summary in ch. 2, and "Pater passus non est" is in ch. 30 (both outside my range, not paged).
- "portio totius" is in ch. 26, not ch. 25.
- Scholar attributions (Bull, Newman, Kelly, Evans, Hanson, etc.) were not checked; no views are attributed to them.
