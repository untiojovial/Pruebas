## Must double-check
1. ANF (Holmes) renders "alium" as "one" in ch. 9 ("the Father is one, and the Son one"). Quote Souter or the Latin.
2. "Tota substantia" / "all being": ANF and Souter differ; ANF is the Latin-faithful one here.
3. Kroymann brackets "oportebat et patrem crucifigi" (ch. 10). Flag it as suspect.
4. Turner's emendation (Souter note): "ita et pater et filius est" (ch. 10) and "ueritatis auctorem" (ch. 11).
5. ANF adds "(Persons)" in ch. 12; the Latin has only "in tribus cohaerentibus".
6. Kroymann's <aliud>, <ut>, <etsi> are his supplements, not MS readings.
7. Orthography: the brief writes "vero" and "derivatio"; the source prints "uero" and "deriuatio".
8. "Minoratus" recurs in ch. 14 (printed p. 25, OCR only).
9. The brief's triad "non statu sed gradu" is in ch. 2 (printed p. 3), not ch. 9-12.
10. I did not search the scholarly defence (Bull, Newman, Kelly, Evans, Osborn, Barnes, Daniélou, Moingt, Waszink, Hanson). Those names are not verified here.
