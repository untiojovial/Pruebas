## Negative or limited results
- "Deum de Deo" is not in Apologeticum 21. The Apology has "de deo deus" and "ut lumen de lumine" (21.12-13). The phrase "deum de deo" occurs only in Ad Nationes 2.3 (T8-16).
- Apol. 21.12 "de spiritu spiritus et de deo deus" is bracketed in the Latin Library (doubt marker); Migne PL 1 prints it unbracketed.
- Virg. 1.4 rule names God, Son and resurrection, not the Spirit. ANF "(as of the spirit)" is a translator's addition.
- Ad Nationes 1-2, De Testimonio Animae, De Resurrectione 24.9, De Oratione 25.2: no Son/Spirit formula found.
- De Anima 16.4, 21.1-4: "trinitas" is the soul's threefold faculties, not the Godhead.
- Adv. Marc. 3.6, 3.9: no Trinitarian content.
- Adv. Marc. 2.27: Latin Library typo "quena" (quem) printed as in source.
