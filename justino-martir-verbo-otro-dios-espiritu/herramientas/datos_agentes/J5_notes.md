# J5 notes: Dialogue with Trypho 56-60 and 126-127

## Searched
- New Advent ANF vol. 1 Dialogue pages 01281-01289 (English, Dods and Reith). Chapters 56-60 read verbatim on 01285, 126-127 on 01289.
- CCEL ANF01 PDF (10.8 MB) in dl/anf01_ccel.pdf; English mapped to PDF and Buffalo 1885 pages.
- Migne PG 6 (1857), archive.org, two OCR copies (version1, version2). Greek for Dial. 56-60 and 126-127 read from version2 and cross-checked against version1 for the key words. The PDF is 84.3 MB, over the 60 MB cap, so it was not downloaded and no PDF page was captured.
- Otto 1847 (Ienae; archive.org siustiniphilosop12just): djvu has no Greek OCR. PDF 69 MB, over cap.
- Otto Corpus vols 1-3 (1969 reprint): no Dialogue Greek in OCR.
- Thirlby 1722, Jebb 1719: Greek OCR sparse, no Dialogue 56-60 or 126-127. PDFs over cap.
- Not opened: earlychristianwritings, Wikisource.

## Negative results
- No usable Greek for Dial. 56-60/126-127 outside PG 6.
- The brief's "heteros theos" (56.4) and "arithmo heteros" (56.11) are not literal phrases. The Greek is "Θεὸς καὶ Κύριος ἕτερος" and "ἕτερός ἐστι ... ἀριθμῷ λέγω, ἀλλὰ οὐ γνώμῃ".
- The brief's section numbers (56.4, 56.11, 126-129) do not occur in any source checked. Refs are chapter-only.
