- No Greek of the sun-ray image is legible in Trollope vol. 2.

## Key findings
- The sun-ray analogy is NOT Justin's own image. ANF 128 reports it as the opponents' view ("the light of the sun"), which Justin rejects. The brief's attribution is wrong.
- The "not by abscission ... essence divided" passage is verified in Otto's quotation of Dial. 128 (J6-13).
- Fire and human-word analogies (61) are Justin's own, verified on page images.
- ANF chapter headings are editorial, not Justin's text.
- The ch. 60 Angel summary (J6-07) is Trypho's speech.
