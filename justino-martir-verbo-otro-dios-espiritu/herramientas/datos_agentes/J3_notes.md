# J3 notes: Second Apology (plus cross-checks)

**Searched**
- ANF English, 2 Apol. (newadvent.org/fathers/0127.htm, Dods and Reith) and 1 Apol. (0126.htm). Both fetched and quoted verbatim.
- Greek: Otto, S. Justini ... opera vol. 1 (Jena: Mauke, 1842), archive.org bib_fict_4102644_1. PDF downloaded to dl/otto1842_v1.pdf (42.7 MB, 400 pp.). Page images rendered and read for PDF pp. 310, 376, 380, 382, 384, 386, 392. Printed page = PDF page minus 80 (consistent on 296, 300, 302, 304, 306, 312).
- BKV Greek (bkv.unifr.ch): connection reset through the proxy, twice. Not retried further.
- Migne PG 6: not identified on archive.org. Items checked and found to be other PG volumes: patrologiaecurs 44, 46, 52, 59, 75, 104, 128.
- Otto Corpus Apologetarum (corpusapologeta00/01/02): not Justin Greek in those scans (00 is an index; 01 Athenagoras; 02 vol. 7).

**Negative results**
- No homoousios or consubstantial in 2 Apol.
- No Holy Spirit in 2 Apol. (Greek and English).
- Logos is not called angel in 2 Apol. Angels appear only as the creation-era wicked angels (ch. 5-6, 9).
- "Partake" (μετέχω), "Logos of whom the whole human race partakes": not in 2 Apol. It is 1 Apol. 46 (J3-14). The brief's attribution is wrong.
- Worship: only 2 Apol. 13.4, προσκυνοῦμεν "after God" (μετὰ τὸν θεόν).
- Only one "begotten" passage in 2 Apol. (ch. 6).
