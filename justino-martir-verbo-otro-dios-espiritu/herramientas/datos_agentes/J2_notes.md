# J2 notes: First Apology 31-68

**Searched and used**
- English: New Advent 0126.htm (ANF vol. 1, Dods and Reith), fetched via the proxy; text checked against each cited chapter.
- Greek: Rauschen 1911 (Florilegium II), archive.org `siustiniapologia00just`. PDF 148 pp., 10.6 MB, text layer present. Page images rendered at 150 dpi in `dl/pg-NNN.png` (PDF pages 72, 74, 88, 96, 104, 106, 108, 110, 112, 114, 116). Eleven pages were image-checked against the Greek.
- Cross-check: Otto 1842 (`bib_fict_4102644_2`) djvu text in `dl/`. The PDF (70 MB) was not downloaded (over the 60 MB limit). Key phrases do not match cleanly, so the cross-check failed.

**Negative results**
- Otto 1851 Corpus (`corpusapologeta00/01unkngoog`) and 1969 facsimiles (`corpusapologeta00/01moragoog`): OCR has no usable Greek.
- `corpusapologeta02unkngoog`: Athenagoras text, not Justin.
- `patrologiaecurs06unkngoog`: Latin content, not PG 6. No PatrologiaGraeca volume 6 identifier found.
- Maran 1742 (`bub_gb_JVckxAdPyRsC`): djvu has zero Greek; PDF 60.3 MB, not downloaded.
- 1768 Greek-Latin (`bim_..._1768`): about 220 Greek characters in OCR; PDF 300 MB, not downloaded.
- 1722 Thirlby: PDF 712 MB, not downloaded.
- "Who is called God" (Jesus) does not occur in 1 Apol. 63 (ANF). The nearest is 63.15 `ϑεὸς ὑπάρχει` for the Word.
- ὁμοούσιος: none in 31-68 Greek.

**Must double-check**
1. 33.4 `ὑπὸ ὁτιοῦν` (scan ambiguous).
2. 63.15 `ὃς` (scan may read `ὡς`).
3. 63.16 `ἐν χρόνοις` (OCR `χρίνοις`).
4. 46.2 `οὗ` and 32.10 `φανησόμενον`.
5. 36.1-2 (item J2-04) is PARTLY: text layer only, not image-checked.
6. ANF renders `εἰκόνι ἀσωμάτῳ` as "likeness of angels" (63.10, 63.16). The Greek does not say angels. ANF also renders βουλή as "counsel" (63.16) and "will" (46.5).
7. Brief's quote for 32 ("first Power ... and Son, is the Word") is a paraphrase; the ANF differs.
