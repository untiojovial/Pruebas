# J4 notes: Dialogue with Trypho 1-55 (+36, 38, 48, 55)

## Searched
- English: New Advent ANF vol. 1 (Dods and Reith), chapters 1-55 read in full for Christ/God/Lord/Spirit/angel/Logos terms (newadvent.org/fathers/01281-01285.htm).
- Greek, used: W. Trollope, 1846 (Cambridge, J. Hall), archive.org `sjustiniphilosop01just`, 168 pp., PDF 11 MB. Greek checked by eye on 260-dpi renders. Printed page = PDF page minus 10.
- Greek, cross-checked only: Otto, Tomus II (1843), archive.org `bib_fict_4102644_2`. OCR is poor for the Greek. The PDF is 70 MB, over the 60 MB limit, so it was not downloaded; no page_file for it.
- Not usable: Otto 1847 (`siustiniphilosop12just`) OCR too garbled; Otto 1876 vol. 2 has no Dialogue; Migne PG 6 not found on archive.org by identifier probes.

## Negative results (chapters 1-55, Greek and English)
- No "first-born" (πρωτότοκος) or "first-begotten" wording.
- No homoousios.
- No "another God" (heteros or allos theos) from Justin's own mouth. The phrase appears only in Trypho's speeches (50, 55).
- "Logos" in 1-55 is ordinary or means Scripture ("ὁ τοῦ Θεοῦ λόγος" = Scripture, ch. 38). No Logos-as-person statement.
- No "second God" or "in the second place".
- Justin's own "born" of Christ is only the virginal birth (43, 48); no temporal "begotten before the ages" claim.
- Old Man speeches (ch. 4, 7) mention the Holy Spirit; these are context, not Justin.

## Discrepancies with the brief
- "another God" at 55: it is Trypho's, and first appears at 50.
- Dial. 56.4 "another God" is outside J4.
- The angel theophanies (Dial. 56-61, 127-128) are outside J4 and were not checked.
