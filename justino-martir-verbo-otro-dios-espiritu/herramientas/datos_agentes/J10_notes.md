## Negative results and corrections
- The brief's "De Decretis 20-24" for "not by will" is wrong: 20-24 narrate the councils. The texts are De Synodis 26.7 and 42.
- Hilary II: no "by will" formula and no "second God"; the answer to "another God" is "One of One" (II.11).
- Novatian: no "by will" and no "second God" (grep).
- Aquinas q.41 a.2 cites "De Synod" for an anathema on a Son "made by the Will of God as a creature". Not found in Athanasius's De Synodis; do not attribute it to him.
- Alexander of Alexandria: no own letter read; only Socrates I.6.
- Not read: other Basil works; Aquinas q.31 a.3, q.42 a.2-3, a.5-6; Augustine II.10-11.
