# J11 notes: Justin in the tradition

**Searched (text read, not just listed):**
- Eusebius HE 4.8-18 (NPNF2 text via newadvent; Greek from Migne PG 20 OCR). HE 4.8 does not name Justin; Justin appears at 4.11, 4.16, 4.18.
- Jerome, De vir. ill. 23 (newadvent, Richardson).
- Irenaeus, Adv. haer. 4.6.2 and 5.26.2 (newadvent, Roberts-Rambaut). Latin not copied.
- Tertullian: Apology, Ad nationes, Adv. Judaeos, De anima, Prescription, Adv. Marcionem, Adv. Praxean (newadvent pages): no mention of Justin in the text. Hits in Adv. Valentinianos 5 and Adv. omnes haereses 7 (ANF vol. 3, PDF-verified).
- Hippolytus, Refutatio (ANF vol. 5 OCR).
- Origen, Contra Celsus and De principiis (ANF vol. 4 OCR): no Justin in Origen's own words; hits are translators' notes.
- Athanasius (newadvent 2801-2821), Basil (3201-3203), Gregory Nazianzen (3102-3103): zero "Justin" hits.
- Newman, Arians; Bull, Defensio vols 1-2 (archive.org).
- ANF vol. 1 pseudo-Justin notes.

**Negative or unverified:**
- No Arian citation of Justin found in what I could read. Asterius: web search only, no Justin citation found. Arius's letters not checked.
- Methodius, Epiphanius, Facundus, Chalcedon: web search only, nothing found. Not read in primary text.
- Photius, Bibliotheca cod. 125: archive copy (Bekker 1824) has no Greek OCR; the English on Project Gutenberg is outside the allowed sources and was not used.
- Athanasius/Basil/Gregory: negative only in newadvent transcriptions.
- Hippolytus names a heretic Justinus (gnostic) and says Tatian was a disciple of "Justinus the Martyr" (J11-08, J11-09). Keep these separate.

**Double-check before use:**
- Eusebius Greek is OCR of Migne PG 20, column not read. HE 4.18.9 Greek renders Irenaeus's Latin.
- Latin of Irenaeus, Jerome, Tertullian: not copied.
- Printed pages for ANF/Newman via OCR running heads; Newman and ANF3 PDF pages checked with pdftotext. Bull vol. 1 page not confirmed.
- Bull translator not confirmed. Newman and Bull English are their own renderings.
- Pseudo-Justin Greek (PG 6 OCR) unusable; English only.
- "Under 60 MB": ANF3 PDF is 61.5 MB decimal (58.7 MiB); used. ANF5 PDF (64 MB) not used.
