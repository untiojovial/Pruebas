**Errors found (verified unless marked):**
- D1: Diognetus 8 says God communicated a "conception" to the Son, not goodness. Chrysostom's quote is about Mt 12:8.
- D3: Adv. Prax. 9 is quoted without "My Father is greater than I" (Jn 14:28).
- D4: Dt 13 tests apostasy to other gods, not law modification.
- D5: Lk 2:21 does not say "para cumplir la ley" (verified). Mk 16:9-20 and Lk 22:43-44 textual problems are not mentioned.
- D7: Q 18:86 reads "maghrib al-shams", not "mawdi' ghurūb" (verified via Tanzil).
- D8: STh III q.15 a.1 is "Whether there was sin in Christ?" (verified). The a.2 argument rests on virtue, not on conception.
- D10: Justin 2 Apol. 6 "es Dios" not found. Named refusers (Teognis, Maris) doubtful. Dionysius' "homoousios" contested.
- D12: table cites "Flm 2:8" for Flp 2:8.
