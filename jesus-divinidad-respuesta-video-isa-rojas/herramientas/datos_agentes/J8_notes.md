# J8 notes: Brown, Hengel, Moule, Ratzinger

**Positions.** Brown (1965 article, verified): the NT calls Jesus God in three clear and five probable places; Jesus is never called God in the Synoptics and John never has him say explicitly "I am God". Brown (1994, partial): the title "God" "would be truly distinctive; but Jesus never uses it of himself". Hengel: an early confession presents the crucified man as a pre-existent divine figure. Moule: development explains Christology; little evidence for early Palestinian worship of Jesus. Ratzinger: the Gospels present Jesus as God made visible.

**Brown does not claim** that Jesus explicitly called himself God. He holds that divine identity is implicit, not that Jesus never claimed it.
