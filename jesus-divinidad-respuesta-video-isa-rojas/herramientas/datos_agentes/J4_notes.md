# J4 notes: Richard Bauckham

## Position
The earliest Christians, within strict Jewish monotheism, included Jesus in the unique identity of the one God of Israel (sole Creator and sole Sovereign); he rejects a late Hellenistic origin (J4-06, J4-12). He does not claim the historical Jesus said he was YHWH; his only statement on Jesus' own speech is that Jesus addressed God as "Abba" (J4-13).
