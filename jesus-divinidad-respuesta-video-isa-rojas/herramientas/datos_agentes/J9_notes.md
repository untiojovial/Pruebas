## Positions
- Bock: the Mk 14 charge rests on a claimed share in God's honour (Ps 110, Dan 7). Unverified in his own words; he does not, as read, claim a divine nature.
- Gathercole: Synoptic portrayal of pre-existence and divinity "in the strong sense" (via Perriman). Claim concerns Gospel portrayal, not Jesus' own words.
- Evans: roots of deification in Jesus' words (via Bird). His Mk 13:27 comment infers authority invested in the Son of Man, not identity with God.
- Witherington: unverified.
- Bird: Jesus identified himself as a divine agent with unique authority; at trial he "most likely" claimed Dan 7:13 and enthronement.
