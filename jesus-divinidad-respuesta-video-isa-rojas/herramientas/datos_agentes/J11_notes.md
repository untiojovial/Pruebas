# J11 notes: early worship of Christ and the Gospel reactions

## Overall position (scholars)
- Bauckham (2018 chapter, Georgetown volume): Jesus belongs to "the unique divine identity" of the one God, not a second god; "God" is only very occasionally used of Jesus.
- Hurtado (chapter draft): the innovation was cultic devotion to Jesus, "not as a second deity"; not a claim that Jesus used YHWH's name in the Gospels.
- Ehrman (NPR 2014): the historical Jesus did not call himself God; the divine sayings are in John; he writes "as a historian".
