# J7 notes (Boyarin, Segal, Schafer)

## Position
Schafer: the high Christological titles (firstborn, God incarnate, Son of God, Son of Man, Messiah) are "rooted in Second Temple Judaism" (2020, p. 5). Boyarin: early Christology and the divine Son of Man of Daniel 7 were Jewish discourse; in Mark 2 the Son of Man's authority is "delegated by God" (essay, page unknown). Segal: rabbinic "two powers" reports show a Jewish second-power idea; he says Jesus "may have" associated himself with the Danielic Son of Man (Mk 14:62).
Not claimed: none says Jesus explicitly claimed to be God or YHWH. Schafer disclaims the NT divinity debate (pp. 8-9).
