# J6 notes: N. T. Wright and Richard B. Hays

**Positions.** Wright: Jesus believed his vocation embodied YHWH's return to Zion (Ex Auditu essay, 1998). Hays: each Gospel presents Jesus as the embodied presence of the God of Israel, mainly through scriptural figuration, with Mark more hesitant.
