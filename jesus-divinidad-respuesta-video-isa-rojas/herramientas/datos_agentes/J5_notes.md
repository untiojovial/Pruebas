# J5 notes: Hurtado and Tilling

**Hurtado's position.** Early devotion to Jesus was a "dyadic" pattern inside Jewish monotheism, starting within the first few years and grounded in post-Easter experience. He holds that the historical Jesus did not claim divinity or demand worship (blog 2017-10-09 post and comments). He says John's divine self-claims are the author's retrospective voice.

**What he does not claim.** He does not say the historical Jesus claimed to be YHWH. He also grants that John 1:1 and 20:28 are the NIDB's "unambiguous" cases of "theos", so "only 20:28" is inaccurate.

**Tilling's position (reviews only).** Paul's Christology is divine: the believer's relation to the risen Lord parallels Israel's relation to YHWH. He does not address the historical Jesus.
