# J5 notes: major campaigns

**Searched.** Bukhari texts from the fawazahmed0/hadith-api mirror (jsDelivr, English and Arabic JSON, global numbers 371, 610, 2945, 3043, 3949, 4122, 4197). Qur'an 9:29 and 33:26-27 via api.alquran.cloud (Tanzil Uthmani Arabic, Sahih International). Guillaume, *The Life of Muhammad* (OUP 1955), archive.org PDF `dl/guillaume.pdf` (431 PDF pages, text layer; spreads: even printed page left, odd right; printed 460-464 = PDF 254-256). Ibn Sa'd, *Tabaqat* vol. 2 (English, soebratie.nl). Downloads in `dl/`.

**Negative or limited results.**
- sunnah.com returned 403 to both curl and WebFetch. Bukhari text is from the mirror, not checked on sunnah.com. Bukhari translator is not named in the mirror.
- Al-Waqidi's sariyya count: no English or Arabic text found. Ibn Sa'd's count was found in primary text (27 ghazwat, 47 sariyyat).
- No Arabic for the Ibn Ishaq/Guillaume items: Wüstenfeld/Ibn Hisham Arabic was not located. Arabic is given only for hadith and Qur'an.
- Only PDF p. 464 (Sa'd's judgement) was checked as an image. The other Guillaume quotes come from the OCR text layer and may contain errors. Status PARTLY means this.
- Guillaume's bracketed numbers, e.g. (709), and margin numbers, e.g. 690, appear to be Wüstenfeld pagination. The notes convention was not confirmed from his introduction.

**Count discrepancies.**
- Ibn Ishaq's "27 raids" list (Guillaume 659-660) has about 28 entries including the pilgrimage. The footnote says Ibn Hisham counted the pilgrimage as a raid. Removing it gives 27; this is my count, not the source's.
- Ibn Ishaq lists 38 expeditions and raiding parties (Tabari: 35), and 9 engagements (Ibn Sa'd also gives 9, but his list differs: al-Muraysi' for al-Mustaliq).
- The catapult word is on p. 587 (Thaqif men in Jurash learning the testudo and catapult), not p. 589 as the brief said. The siege narrative is on p. 589.

**Double-check before use.**
- Khaybar wording: "ruined" (371, 610, 2945) vs "destroyed" (4197).
- Mu'ta place names are OCR-garbled and were left out.
- The Ayla/Jarba'/Adhruh treaty (Guillaume 607) is on the right half of PDF 327.
