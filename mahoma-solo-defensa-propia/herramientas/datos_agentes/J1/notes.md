## Negative results
- No Wustenfeld Ibn Hisham scan found on archive.org. All Arabic is from the Saqqa edition.
- Rabigh is not named in the 'Ubayda passage; Guillaume gives "water in the Hijaz below Thaniyyatu'l-Murra". "Rabigh" appears only in a geographic footnote (PDF p. 43).
- No clean Arabic found for Buwat, al-'Ushayra, or the killing of 'Amr b. al-Hadrami.
