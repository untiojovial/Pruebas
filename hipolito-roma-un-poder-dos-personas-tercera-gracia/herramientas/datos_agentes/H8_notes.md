# H8 notes: identity and context of Hippolytus

## Searched
- Eusebius HE 5, 6 (newadvent NPNF2 McGiffert; Greek OCR of Migne PG 20, archive.org). Greek is OCR only and largely illegible, so English is the verified text.
- Jerome Vir. ill. 61 and 69 (newadvent NPNF2 Richardson). Latin of 61 not obtained.
- Photius Bibl. 48, 121 (Freese 1920, Gutenberg 79139; Henry Budé vol. 2 on archive.org, PDF p. 90 OCR'd with tesseract). Greek of cod. 121 not obtained.
- Liber Pontificalis and Depositio martyrum (Duchesne 1886, archive.org duchesne01, OCR). Printed page numbers from the OCR headers.
- Chronography 354 (tertullian.org partial transcription), via Lietzmann RE (de.wikisource). Mommsen not opened.
- ANF 5 intro (ccel), Schaff HCC vol. 2 § 183 (ccel), Schaff-Herzog 'Hippolytus' (ccel), BMCR review of Cerrato 2002 (2003.10.02).

## Negative results
- Eusebius HE 6.20-22 names no see and does not name Rome.
- Eusebius HE 5.28 names no author for the anti-Artemon work (the Little Labyrinth).
- Chronography 354 Depositio episcoporum (255-352) does not list Hippolytus.
- Damasus carm. 37 (Ihm): not obtained, memory-only.
- Cerrato cites Eusebius HE 6.39.1f. for Hippolytus. I found no Hippolytus in the McGiffert text of ch. 39. Unresolved.
