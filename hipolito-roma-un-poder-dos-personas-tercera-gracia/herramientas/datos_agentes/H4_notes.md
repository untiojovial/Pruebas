# H4 notes: Refutatio X.32-34 (Hippolytus's confession)

**Searched:** newadvent ANF 5 Book X (MacMahon, read verbatim); archive.org Miller 1851 Oxford Greek (origenisphilosop00hipp, PDF read at 200 dpi, printed pp. 334-339 = PDF 352-357); archive.org ANF 5 (antenicenefather05robe, 1919 Scribner, for page numbers); Legge 1921 scan located (not used); WebSearch for Wendland/Marcovich/Greek phrases.

**Negative / not found:**
- "when it seemed good to Him He created the world": not in the text. Nearest: X.33 "Ὅτε δὲ (ἡ) ὡς ἠθέλησε καὶ ἐποίησεν" (H4-09).
- "you shall become a god" is not verbatim; the text has "εἰ δὲ θέλεις καὶ θεὸς γενέσθαι" (X.33, H4-07) and "Γέγονας γὰρ θεός" (X.34, H4-13).
- "the Logos ... Logos is God, being the substance" is X.33 (ANF 29), not X.32.
- Lagarde 1858 (hippolytiromaniq0000paul): archive.org returns 401 (lending). Miller djvu text and the Google scan: Greek OCR unusable, so I read the page images instead.
- No free Wendland GCS 26 or Marcovich scan found. Wikisource pages 404. Duncker-Schneidewin not found.
- Brief says Refutatio is in PG 10; Miller is not Migne. PG volume not verified.
