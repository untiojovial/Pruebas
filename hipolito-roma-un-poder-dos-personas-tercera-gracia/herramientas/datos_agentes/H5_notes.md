# H5 notes (Hippolytus: Daniel, Christ and Antichrist, other works)

## Searched and read
- New Advent ANF 5 Hippolytus pages 0501-0510 (Daniel fragments in 0502, translator S.D.F. Salmond; Exegetical Fragments).
- CCEL ANF05 pages for Christ and Antichrist (pp. 204-219) and Hippolytus sections (pp. 163-252). Printed ANF page numbers come from CCEL page markers.
- GCS 1 (Bonwetsch-Achelis 1897), archive.org "hippolytuswerke01hipp" djvu OCR: Greek of Daniel (p. 106) and De Antichristo (running heads).
- Migne PG 10, archive.org "patrologiaecursu10mignuoft" djvu OCR: Greek cross-check of Daniel 3 and the proem; Latin interleaved, so less reliable.

## Negative or incomplete
- Not read: Blessings of Isaac and Jacob as a complete work; In Cantica (Song of Songs fragments, ANF p. 176 region); De Pascha (ANF "Homily on the Lord's Pasch", p. ~240, and GCS "Περὶ τοῦ ἁγίου πάσχα"). Not verified in this run.
- Slavonic Commentary on Daniel: no English translation read; not checked.
- Christ and Antichrist c.6: Greek I read says "τοῦ υἱοῦ τοῦ θεοῦ" (Son of God); ANF renders "who is also God". Do not cite "Christ is God" from c.6 until the GCS apparatus or Migne text is checked.
- Brief's "4.11, fiery furnace" does not match the text: the furnace passage is Dan 3 (LXX 3:92-93).
- Authenticity status (Christ and Antichrist, Daniel, Blessings, Cantica, Pascha) is not verified here; treat as UNVERIFIED. ANF "Appendix to the Works of Hippolytus: Dubious and Spurious Pieces" (pp. 242-252) read at heading level only.

## Double-check
- GCS page numbers for Christ and Antichrist c.3, c.4, c.26 are inferred from OCR running heads (c.26 probably p. 18). Only Dan 3:92 (p. 106) and c.61 (p. 41) are clear.
- OCR errors: κ/χ, θ/ϑ, and ἢ/ἡ are confused in the GCS and PG text. Greek is as read; verify against the printed page before publishing.
- Daniel ANF English is not pinned to printed pages (pp. 178-192).
- "Declared to be God and man" (ANF c.61, p. 217) not located in the Greek I read.
