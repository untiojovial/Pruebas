# H3 notes: Refutatio IX.7-12 (Noetus, Epigonus, Cleomenes, Heraclitus, Callistus)

## Searched
- Greek: Duncker-Schneidewin 1859 (archive.org refutationisomn00hippgoog). Its PDF is image-only (no text layer), so pdftotext cannot confirm pages. I read the Greek from 130-dpi renders of PDF pages 451, 453, 459, 461, 463, 467, 469 (Duncker pp. 440, 442, 448, 450, 452, 456, 458). Pages 455 and 465 were not checked visually.
- Miller 1851 (archive.org origenisphiloso00origgoog_text.pdf) has a text layer but its Greek OCR is badly garbled. I used it only as a cross-check for the Miller page parentheses.
- English: ANF vol. 5 text (newadvent 050109, J. H. MacMahon) and ANL vol. 6 (1868, archive.org djvu OCR, same translation). ANL page numbers come from the OCR page headers.
- Not used: the Duncker parenthetical "(p. 278 ...)" is assumed to cite Miller pagination; not checked independently.

## Negative results
- No Greek text in this assignment supports a Trinitarian reading in the sense of distinct persons. All 17 items are Hippolytus' reports of monarchian (Noetian/Callistian) doctrine or his polemic. Trinitarian material must come from C. Noet. 14 and Ref. 10.33, which are not in H3.
- The brief's "Noetus (9.7-10)" is only partly right: IX.7 has the Noetus origin; IX.8-10 is the Heraclitus excursus.

## Double-check
1. Item 05: the Greek "λέγει" has no explicit subject. ANF renders "Noetus"; ANL brackets "[Noetus]" as a translator's insertion; the Latin and the preceding sentence point to Heraclitus. Attribution unresolved.
2. Item 02 and 17: Greek letters read at 130 dpi ("Οὗ" vs "Ὁ"; cemetery text). Verify on the original.
3. Item 03 and 05: accents ("Ζεφυρίνου", "ηὐδόκησεν") are as read from image.
4. Items 13 and 10: English is abridged or fragmentary; check the full sentence.
5. ANF vol. 5 printed page numbers are unknown; ANL vol. 6 pages are from OCR headers.
6. Item 17 (cemetery): the identification with the Via Appia catacomb is an ANL editorial note, not checked.
