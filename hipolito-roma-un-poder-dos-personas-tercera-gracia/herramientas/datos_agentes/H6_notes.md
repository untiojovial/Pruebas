# H6 notes: Apostolic Tradition (attribution disputed)

## Searched
- Dix 1937 (archive.org: treatiseonaposto0000greg, treatiseonaposto0000unse, apostolictraditi0000greg): metadata shows access-restricted lending copies. Not downloaded or read. No Dix text is quoted.
- Brent 1995, Bradshaw-Johnson-Phillips 2002: no open text reached in the searches made. Their views are not summarized.
- Cooper & Maclean 1902 (Testament of Our Lord, English): not found on archive.org or in web search.
- ANF 5 index (newadvent 0501-0504): no Apostolic Tradition among Hippolytus' works.

## Found and read
- Hauler 1900 (Verona Latin), Tattam 1848 (Coptic + English), Horner 1904 (Ethiopic and Sahidic + English), Rahmani 1899 (Latin), Achelis 1891 (German), ANF 7 Apostolic Constitutions VII (newadvent), Eusebius HE 6.22 and Jerome Vir. ill. 61 (newadvent), Schaff/CCEL statue entry.

## Findings
- The Verona Latin is the Egyptian Canons (Hauler LXXIII), a related church order. The brief's claim that Verona preserves the AT is inaccurate as stated.
- No Greek of AT 21 survives. No Greek is quoted.
- "Of the same essence/substance" appears in Tattam (Coptic) and Horner (Sahidic). It is absent from the Ethiopic ("equal in Godhead"). Its date is not established here.
- Achelis treats the filioque in the Canones creed as a later addition. The Canones are not the AT.
- The doxology (AT 4) and blessing of oil (AT 5) are not read in any source. Horner's introduction (xiii) only compares the Oblation prayer.
