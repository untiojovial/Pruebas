# H1 notes: Contra Noetum ch. 1-8

**Searched.** Lagarde 1858 Greek (archive.org hippolytiromani00lagagoog; PDF pages 64-71 = printed pp. 43-50, read as 220 dpi images and checked against the OCR layer). Migne PG 10 (archive.org patrologiaecursu10mignuoft OCR, used for ch. 1-4 and Migne's notes only). ANF vol. 5 text from CCEL (anf05.iii.iv.ii.iv.html) and printed pages from archive.org "Ante Nicene Fathers, Vol 5" OCR. Files in dl/.

**Negative results.** newadvent 0501-0511 (Refutation, Exegetical Fragments 0502, Cyprian, etc.): no Against Noetus. Wikisource guessed title: 404. earlychristianwritings: 406. PG 10 PDF is 79 MB, over the 60 MB limit, so only its OCR text was used. No Greek text of Noetus found in open PDFs beyond Lagarde and PG 10.

**Brief corrections.** The "Father sends the Son" sentence is not in ch. 8; "sent" (πεμφθείς) is in ch. 7. ch. 8 gives "threefold manifestation" (τριχῆς ἡ ἐπίδειξις) with "one power". "Two persons" (δύο πρόσωπα) is in ch. 7 (H1-20), not only ch. 14 (ch. 14 not checked: outside assignment).

**Double-check before use.**
1. H1-13: Lagarde prints "λόγος σὰρξ ἦν" (image checked); ANF English says only "Word, Spirit, Power". Migne PG 10 note (11): "Λόγος γὰρ ἦν. Male apud Fabricium, Λόγος σὰρξ ἦν." The ANF follows the Migne reading. Status PARTLY.
2. H1-16: Greek γεγένηται; ANF "has been born" is loose. Status PARTLY.
3. Accents on some words were read from a 220 dpi image; the OCR layer agrees on most words, not on every diacritic. Lagarde's apparatus (R = Routh, cod. = codex) is recorded in limits where it matters (e.g. H1-20 ἐπεὶ/ἐπί, ἐσμέν/ἐστιν).
4. Migne PG 10 column numbers are approximate (OCR page headers only, not checked on image).
5. Translator "S. D. F. Salmond" comes from the ANF vol. 5 section heading in the archive.org OCR, not from the CCEL page, which gives no translator line.
6. "Power" (δύναμις, H1-13) and "ἀγέννητος" (H1-26) are terms Arian and Unitarian readers will cite.
