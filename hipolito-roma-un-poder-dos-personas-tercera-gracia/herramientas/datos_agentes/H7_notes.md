# H7 notes (disputed and spurious works, fragments)

**Searched:** newadvent.org (ANF 5 Hippolytus 0501-0504); Wikisource ANF 5 subpages (contents only, text on sub-subpages, not fully read); CCEL anf05 full text (cache anf05.txt, read for Against Plato, Beron and Helix, Theophany, Fragments, Appendix); archive.org Lagarde 1858 (OCR and text-layer PDF, dl/lagarde1858_text_layer.pdf, 19.8 MB); Migne PG 10 (OCR only, PDF 79 MB not downloaded); Migne PG 20 OCR; Photius Myriobiblon Rothomagi 1653 OCR.

**Found (Trinitarian content):** Beron and Helix frags IV and V (Christology; "tetras" vs "trias", where "Trinity" is ANF's gloss, not Greek); Theophany ch. 8 and doxology (Father, Son/Word, Spirit); pseudo-Hippolytus Eschatology ("co-eternal with the Spirit", doxology); Canons of Alexandria Canon I (explicit Trinity, ANF heading "Wrongly ascribed to Hippolytus"); Doubtful Pentateuch preface (formula, Syriac-tradition text).

**Negative:** Twelve and Seventy Apostles, Heads of Canons (no Father/Son/Spirit formula in the ranges scanned). Against Plato is about Hades, with no Trinitarian content. "Discourse to the Greeks concerning Hades" is not an ANF 5 title.

**Not done / unverified:** Photius Bibl. 121 (OCR unusable); Eusebius HE 6.22 (no Hippolytus match in PG 20 OCR); Jerome DVI 61 (not read); Theodoret Haer. fab. not searched.
