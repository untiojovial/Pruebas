# G1 notes: Africanus and Eusebius

**Searched:** Loeb 1926 vol. 1 (Lake; Greek = Schwartz per its preface), PDF pp. 114-124 read as images (HE 1.7.1-17). NPNF2 vol. 1 HE book I (newadvent, McGiffert). ANF vol. 6 Africanus page. Migne PG 22 Epitome (Quaestiones ad Stephanum), PDF pp. 469-479, read as images. Spitta 1877 (PDF p. 90, text layer only). Routh vol. 2 (text layer only). Downloaded to dl/: Loeb, Spitta, Routh, Gelzer 1880, PG 22 PDFs and OCR text. PG 20 OCR downloaded, not used. OCR layers were too noisy to quote; used only to locate pages.

**Negative results:**
1. No text read says Heli/Eli was Mary's father, or that Luke's genealogy is Mary's.
2. Africanus's letter (HE, Epitome, Routh) never mentions Mary. The only Mary sentence in HE 1.7 is Eusebius's (1.7.17): "virtually" (δυνάμει) the same tribe, not the house of David.
3. Epitome Quaestio I says no account shows Mary descended from David (PG 22 approx. col. 881). It then argues Mary's Davidic line from Joseph's house (Lk 2:4-5) and Gabriel's words, not from Luke's list.
4. The ANF "Narrative of events in Persia" gives only Mary's place (Bethlehem). Its authorship is rejected in the Migne preface quoted by ANF.
