# G4 notes: the curse of Jeconiah

## Searched
- Sefaria API: Jer 22:24-30, 36:30; 1 Chr 3:15-19; 2 Kgs 24:8-9, 25:27-30; Hag 2:23; Ezra 3:2; Targum Jonathan Jer 22; Pesikta de-Rav Kahana 24:11; Vayikra Rabbah 10:5; Sanhedrin 37b-38a.
- Irenaeus AH III.21.9: English from New Advent (ANF 1). Latin from Harvey vol. 2 (1857), PDF p. 130 = printed p. 119, checked on the image.
- Greek: Westcott-Hort 1881 (archive.org), checked on the image: Matt 1:11-12 PDF p. 14 (printed p. 4); Luke 3:27-28 PDF p. 134 (printed p. 124).
- Jerome PL 26 (archive.org): OCR only, because the PDF is 63 MB.

## Results
- Mt 1:12: Jechonias begat Shealtiel. Lk 3:27: Shealtiel son of Neri. Same names, different fathers. 1 Chr 3:19 gives Zerubbabel son of Pedaiah, while Ezra 3:2 and Hag 1:1 say son of Shealtiel. State this tension.
- Irenaeus III.21.9 (c. 180) says Matthew "sets forth" Joseph's pedigree through Joachim and Jechoniah. Matthew = Joseph is attested in the 2nd century. Irenaeus does not mention Luke or Mary.
- Pesikta 24:11, Vayikra Rabbah 10:5 and Sanhedrin 37b: repentance or exile softens the curse; none says it is fully lifted.
- Jerome, Matt commentary (OCR): Jechonias = Joachim; Joseph and Mary of one tribe and stock (c. 400). No explicit Davidic claim for Mary.

## Negative results
- Pirkei de-Rabbi Eliezer 49-50: no Jeconiah forms.
- No Greek text of Irenaeus Book III; Harvey's "θνησιμαῖον" is a gloss.
- Not reached: Ambrose, Augustine, Jerome on Jer 22.
