# G2 notes: who first said Luke gives Mary's line?

**Result:** no pre-1500 text I read says Luke's genealogy is Mary's, or that Heli is Mary's father. Nearest: Bede (c. 710s) says Joseph's list shows Mary's origin, since they shared one stock. Damascene (c. 745) gives Mary a separate Davidic list but says Luke is Joseph's.

**Read:** Eusebius HE I.7 (English); Eusebius, Ad Stephanum IV-V (PG 22 OCR; PDF pp. 477, 479, 480 viewed); Ambrose, Exp. Luc. III.15 (PL 15 OCR); Augustine, De consensu II.4.11 (English); Contra Faustum XXIII.4 (PL 42 OCR); Bede, In Lucam II (PL 92 OCR); Damascene DFO IV.14 (English); Aquinas ST III q31 a3 (English); Protevangelium 10; ANF6 Africanus note; Jerome Ep. 120 (no genealogy question).

**Negative or incomplete:** Gregory Naz. Or. 38 (PG 36 OCR: no Heli statement); Epiphanius Panarion (PG 42 OCR: no Joachim hit, poor OCR); Annius 1515 reprint (index only); Hilary In Matthaeum (not located); Augustine Quaestiones evangeliorum (not located); Machen and Godet (secondary only).

**Not checked:** Brown, Marshall, Plummer, Johnson, Lyra, Glossa ordinaria, Theophylact, Euthymius, Rupert, Albert, Gerson, Catena Aurea, Aphrahat, Ephrem, Ad Marinum, the Talmud "Miriam daughter of Eli".
