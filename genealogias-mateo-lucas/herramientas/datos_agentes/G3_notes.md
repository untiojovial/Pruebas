# G3 notes: Jewish sources and law

**Read (Sefaria API, saved in raw/):** y. Hagigah 2:2; y. Sanhedrin 6:6; Bava Batra 109b; Yevamot 62b; Sanhedrin 19b; Mishnah Bava Batra 8:6; Mishnah Kiddushin 3:12; Numbers 27:1-11 and 36:1-9; 1 Chronicles 2:34-35; Ezra 2:61-62; Nehemiah 7:63; Deuteronomy 25:5-10; Ruth 4:1-10; Jeremiah 23:5-6; Maimonides, Kings and Wars 11:1.

**Page scans (dl/):** Gluck Hebrew Hagigah (Miriam on PDF p.30, viewed as image; "until the son of David" on p.29). Gluck Sanhedrin (Miriam on p.45, text layer only). Lightfoot, Horae Hebraicae vol. 3 (Heli passage on PDF p.61, printed p.55, viewed as image).

**Positive results**
- Miriam daughter of Eli appears in y. Hagigah 2:2 and y. Sanhedrin 6:6 (Sefaria numbering). Neither text mentions Mary, Heli, Joseph, David or Jesus.
- Earliest identification of this Miriam with Mary daughter of Heli that I found: Lightfoot (Horae vol. 3, p.55, footnote citing "Hieros. Chagigah, fol. 77.4"). Britannica 1842 and 1860 repeat it.

**Negative results**
- Yevamot 62b: no family-line or Davidic statement.
- Sefaria's "6:9" page is not the Miriam story; the brief's 6:9 is not reconciled. Its English segments look misaligned, so I did not use it.
- No Mary=Heli witness before 1674 found by WebSearch. Not searched: Wagenseil, earlier Christian commentators, Toledot Yeshu.
- 1959 Bavli Sanhedrin PDF returned 401 (restricted); no PD Sanhedrin 19b scan captured.

**Limits**
- Lublin 1576 Bava Batra PDF is 74 MB (not downloaded); djvu OCR is garbled, used only for partial confirmation.
- Vilna 1922 Hagigah PDF is 270 MB (not downloaded); djvu OCR only.
- Britannica entries are HTML transcriptions (no originals; 0-word original).
- Lightfoot's Hebrew quote is 13 words, below the 15-word minimum.
