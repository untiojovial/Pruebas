# G7 notes (patristic and medieval Mary-of-David witnesses)

**Searched and read, English verified (ccel/newadvent, PDF captured):** Ignatius Eph 18, 20; Trall 9; Smyrn 1; Justin Dial 43, 45, 100, 120; Irenaeus III.16.2, III.21.5, III.21.9; Tertullian De carne Christi 21, Adv. Marc. V.8; Protevangelium 10.1; Africanus via Eusebius HE I.7; Augustine De consensu II.2.4, II.3.5, II.3.29; Contra Faustum XXIII.4, .8; Chrysostom Hom. Mt I.14, II.7; Jerome Adv. Helvidius; Damascene DF IV.14; Aquinas ST III q31 a2. Latin read from Migne OCR: Tertullian PL 2, Augustine PL 34, Ambrose PL 15, Chrysostom PG 57 (Montfaucon Latin).

**Negative results:**
- Justin Dial 100 has no Mary or David; Dial 120 has "through the Virgin Mary" with no David. The brief's wording for these chapters is not in the text.
- Africanus, Augustine, Damascene: none says Luke's list is Mary's. Damascene says Matthew and Luke "both pass in silence" on the Virgin's origin.
- No pre-1500 witness in my set says Luke 3:23-38 is Mary's line or that Heli was Mary's father.
- Talmud "Mary daughter of Eli" (NPNF1 6 editor's note citing Lightfoot): not checked, UNVERIFIED.
- Not reached: Augustine Retractationes II.16; Origen (Hom. Luke 28, Comm. Rom. 1.3); Eusebius Ad Marinum; Hilary (no Mt 1 section found in PL 9 OCR); Bede; Epiphanius; Theophylact; Lyra; Catena Aurea.
