# G6 notes: Luke 3:23-38 and the Davidic texts

## Searched
- Greek: Westcott-Hort 1881 line on Biblehub (Lk 1:26-36, 2:3-5, 3:23-38; Rom 1:3-4; Acts 2:30-31, 13:23; 2 Tim 2:8-9; Heb 7:13-14; Rev 22:16-17; John 7:42-43). Nestle 1904, Tischendorf 8th and TR variants on the same page. No open scan of W-H or NA was read.
- LXX: Swete 1887 vol. 1 (archive.org, PDF p. 49, OCR text layer absent, read as djvu OCR); Rahlfs 1935 vol. I (djvu OCR); Brenton 1851 (PDF text layer, p. 15).
- Hebrew: Sefaria (Masoretic text and JPS English).
- Scans read: Plummer ICC Luke (PDF pp. 115, 117, 119, 197); Godet vol. 1 (1875, pp. 198-202); Robertson Luke (p. 46); Machen (pp. 127, 204). Text layers usable except Swete.
- Eusebius HE 1.7 (McGiffert, newadvent); Augustine De consensu 2.3.5-6 (Salmond, newadvent).

## Negative results
- Talmud "Miriam daughter of Heli/Eli": absent from Sefaria texts of JT Hagigah 2:2 and 2:1, BT Hagigah 4b, BT Sanhedrin 106a, BT Ketubot 66a. Godet's "Chagiga 77.4" not located.
- Africanus, Eusebius and Augustine do not say Mary is Heli's daughter.
- Eusebius HE 1.7 Greek not found in the Schwartz volumes I could reach.
- Bauckham 1990: lending-restricted (HTTP 401). Marshall NIGTC and Lightfoot not found in open form.
- Brenton's 11:13 prints "Canaan" for Cainan (misprint).
