# G8 notes: modern scholarship and objections

## Searched (open scans; PDF page numbers checked in dl/)

- Lightfoot, Horae Hebraicae, Pitman 1859, vols I-II.
- Robertson 1922; Machen 1930; Strauss, NY 1860, tr. Marian Evans; Godet, Luke I, 1875. PDFs in dl/.
- Edersheim vol. I, 1915 copy: OCR text only.

- Sefaria: Jer 22:30 (Hebrew; JPS 1917); Jerusalem Talmud Sanhedrin 6:9; Bavli Sanhedrin 44b, 45b.
- biblehub interlinear: Greek of Lk 1:5, 1:36, 3:23; Mt 1:16.

## Negative results
- Pseudo-Jerome tract (De ortu...): text not found; Heli claim known only via Maldonatus, quoted by Hervey p. 354.
- Talmud "Mary daughter of Eli": not in Sefaria Hebrew of Yer. Sanh. 6:9 or Bav. Sanh. 44b-45b. It appears only in Lightfoot's own rendering (p. 94, "unless my conjecture fail me").
- Galatinus and Annius: no primary source opened; Hervey and Robertson are secondhand.
- Luther as Lk=Mary: named by Mill (via Hervey) and Robertson; Luther's text not checked.
- Augustine: De cons. II.3.5 gives adoption, not Lk=Mary; Retractations not checked; Maldonatus's "Nec Augustino displicet" not checked.
- Edersheim: no genealogy appendix found in vol. I; only pp. 148-149 read.
- Brown, Johnson, Fitzmyer, Marshall, Ehrman, Bauckham, Orr: not opened (not open scans); no quotes given.
- Bauer (1909) and Burkitt (1904): quoted only as Machen quotes them.
