# G5 notes: Matthew 1:1-25

## Searched and read
- Greek: Westcott-Hort 1881 text (archive.org newtestamentinor1881west.pdf), read as page images, PDF pp. 13-15 at 300 dpi. Verified 1:1-25 as printed.
- Hebrew (Sefaria, Masoretic text): 1 Chr 3:10-19; 2 Kings 8:24-26, 14:1-3, 23:34, 24:6; Jer 22:30, 36:30; Gen 46:12, 46:18, 46:25, 46:27.
- Augustine, De consensu II.3.5-7 (NPNF1-06 English). Retractations II.16 only via an NPNF1-06 footnote.
- Eusebius HE I.7 (NPNF2-01 English, sections 1-8).
- Aquinas, Catena Aurea on Mt 1:16 (English, Jerome quoted).
- Chrysostom Hom. II on Mt 1:1 (NPNF1-10 English).
- Sinaitic Syriac Mt 1:16: Lewis 1894 English translation (OCR). Syriac scan garbled.
